# Excess ETH is stuck in the contract and users cannot withdraw it

**Summary**
`GatewaySend.depositAndCall` receives ETH and calls `gateway.depositAndCall` through the `_handleETHDeposit` to send out ETH with the amount of `amount`. However, when `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` receives ETH, it only requires that the amount of ETH is greater than or equal to `amount`, which makes it possible for users to send ETH greater than `amount`, of which `amount` is transferred away, and the ETH amount of `msg.value - amount` will be stuck in the contract and cannot be recovered, causing losses to users.

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-dodo-cross-chain-dex/blob/main/omni-chain-contracts/contracts/GatewaySend.sol#L287-L308

**Impact**
When a user calls `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` and accidentally pays an amount of ETH exceeding `amount`, the excess ETH will be stuck in the contract and cannot be withdrawn, causing losses to the user.

**Mitigation**
Change `require(msg.value >= amount)` to `require(msg.value == amount)`.
