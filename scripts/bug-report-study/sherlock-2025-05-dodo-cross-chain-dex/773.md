# Users can exploit the token amounts of others in `GatewayTransferNative.onCall` due to a mismatch between `decoded.targetZRC20` and `params.toToken`, enabling them to execute a dummy swap and misappropriate the `decoded.targetZRC20` token.

## Vulnerability Details 

The `GatewayTransferNative.onCall` function can be invoked through the universal Gateway when a user utilizes the EVM-compatible `GatewaySend` to deposit assets and designates `GatewayTransferNative` as the target contract. During this process, the user can specify `decoded.targetZRC20`, which is derived from the `_message` prepared during the `GatewaySend` call and provided by the user through `GatewaySend.depositAndCall`. Simultaneously, the user can also define a `MixSwapParams.params.toToken`, which represents the output token that `GatewayTransferNative` will receive after executing the swap for the user-supplied token before transferring it to their Zetachain address.

The problem occurs due to the absence of checks ensuring that `params.toToken` = `decoded.targetZRC20`. This oversight permits any caller to forfeit their initial swap from `params.fromToken` to `params.toToken` and simply claim funds from the `GatewayTransferNative` contract that are intended for refunds. Consequently, the amount retrieved can exceed the amount the user initially forfeited in the swap.

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice transfers 1 USDC (1e6) from Ethereum to her Zetachain address through the following process:
    - GatewaySend.depositAndCall -> GatewayTransferNative.onCall -> TransferHelper.safeTransfer -> her address on Zetachain
    - In the GatewaySend.depositAndCall function, she sets up the `payload` with decoded.targetZRC20 = WBTC.ETH, params.fromToken = USDC.ETH, and params.toToken = USDT.ETH.
2. She observes that the GatewayTransferNative contract holds 1e6 worth of `WBTC.ETH` designated for refunds.
3. She successfully goes through the `GatewayTransferNative._doMixSwap` process, resulting in 1e6 USDT.ETH being sent to `GatewayTransferNative`.
4. However, because she specified `decoded.targetZRC20 = WBTC.ETH`, the `1e6` worth of `WBTC.ETH` in the contract is instead transferred to her Zetachain address.
5. Ultimately, Alice loses her 1e6 worth of `USDC.ETH` (which was swapped for `USDT.ETH`) but receives 1e6 worth of `WBTC.ETH`, placing the protocol at a disadvantage.

## Root Cause

Lack of checks to ensure intended token to receive on zeta chain is same as swapped token

## Impact

Loss of funds for protocol. Insolvency.

## Mitigation

Check `params.toToken = decoded.targetZRC20` in the `GatewayTransferNative.onCall` flow in the else block.
