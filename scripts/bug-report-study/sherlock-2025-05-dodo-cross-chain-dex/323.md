# Static `gasLimit` decided by protocol can cause user to lose funds

## Vulnerability Details

When a user withdraws tokens from Zetachain to their designated address on the destination chain using `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain`, they incur a gas fee in the form of the corresponding `gasZRC20` token linked to the `ZRC20` token.

The issue arises, however, when the `gasLimit` is set as a fixed value determined by the protocol. This can lead to situations where, if the `gasLimit` is modified, users may end up receiving fewer tokens than anticipated on the destination chain.

In `GatewayTransferNative._handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw`

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice intends to transfer 100 USDC.ETH to her Ethereum receiver address using `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain`, which leads her into `GatewayTransferNative._handlEvmOrSolanaWithdraw`, where she observes that the gasLimit is set to 5e6. Let's assume the gas costs 1 USDC.ETH in gasZRC20 tokens.
2. Alice's transaction is currently pending execution.
3. The owner adjusts the `gasLimit` to 10e6. Assume this gas now costs 2 USDC.ETH.
4. When Alice's transaction finally executes and enters `GatewayTransferNative._handlEvmOrSolanaWithdraw`, the gasFee amounts to 2 USDC.ETH. Although she has sufficient funds to cover this fee, she only sends 98 USDC.ETH, which is then received as USDC at her Ethereum receiver address.
5. Ultimately, she ends up receiving less USDC (98 vs 99) than she originally intended

## Root Cause

Static `gasLimit` causes users to lose funds.

## Impact

Loss of funds for the user

## Mitigation

Allow user to specify the `gasLimit` as a parameter during withdrawals of tokens to destination chain.
