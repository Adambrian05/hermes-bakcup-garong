# Wrong withdrawal amount to the destination chain in `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain` ->  `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw` calls due to incorrect deduction of `gasFee`

## Vulnerability Details

When a user initiates a withdrawal to the destination chain (Bitcoin), they follow the call from `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain` -> `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw`. However, an issue arises where the actual amount intended for withdrawal to the specified destination chain receiver address is wrongly decremented by the `gasFee`.

This occurs because the protocol incorrectly deducts the `gasFee` from the total amount the user expects to receive on the destination chain (Bitcoin). Since gas prices can vary, this results in the user potentially receiving significantly less funds on the destination chain than they originally intended.

## POC

Let's consider the following example:

1. Alice plans to withdraw 100 USDC to her Bitcoin chain receiver address using `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain`.
2. After deducting platform fees, this leaves her with 98 USDC.
3. If the gas token is also USDC ZRC20, the gas fee is 1 USDC.
4. This 1 USDC will be transferred to `GatewayZEVM` and subsequently burned through the `GatewayZEVM._burnProtocolFees` function.
5. Consequently, Alice will receive only 97 USDC on the destination chain instead of the expected 98 USDC.

## Root Cause

Users amount to withdraw to destination chain is wrongly decremented by `gasFee`

## Impact

Users receives less funds than intended. Loss of funds for users given `gasPrice` can change

## Mitigation
Consider making the to cover the `gasFee` in addition to the amount they intend to withdraw at the time of calling `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain`, rather than deducting it from the intended withdrawal amount.
