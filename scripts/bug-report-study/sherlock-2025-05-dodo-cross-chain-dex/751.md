# Hardcoding `abortAddress == address(0)` results in having no `onAbort` functionality

### Summary

Hardcoding `abortAddress` to `address(0)` is incorrect, as ZetaChain's flow, will attempt to transfer the funds to the `abortAddress` and then call `onAbort` on it, so it can be handled correctly by the Universal App. [ZetaChain reference](https://github.com/zeta-chain/protocol-contracts/blob/a2b6c07814ed108fcba4f2f124757bf45af2f8aa/contracts/zevm/GatewayZEVM.sol#L497-L513)

This happens in both `withdrawAndCall` & `withdraw`.

`onAbort` will be called in two cases:
1. During an Incoming message (Connected Chain -> ZetaChain) and `onRevert` fails on the connected chain.
2. During an Outgoing message (ZetaChain -> Connected Chain) and `onRevert` fails on ZetaChain.

You can read more about how revert and abort are handled [here](https://www.zetachain.com/docs/developers/chains/zetachain/#revert-transactions).

### Root Cause

Hardcoding `abortAddress` in `GatewayCrossChain` and `GatewayTransferNative` to `address0` when constructing `RevertMessage`
