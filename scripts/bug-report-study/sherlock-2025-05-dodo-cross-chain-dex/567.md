# Wrong approval amount in `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw` allows user to spend excess amount approved via `GatewayTransferNative.withdraw`

## Vulnerability Details 

In `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw`, when a user intends to send assets to their designated receiver address on the Bitcoin network, they follow the flow from `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain` to `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw`.

The issue occurs when an amount greater than intended is approved through the `_handleBitcoinWithdraw` function, allowing malicious actors to utilize the excess allowance amount, which is equivalent to `outputAmount - gasFee`, via `GatewayTransferNative.withdraw`. These funds would be drawn from the reserves allocated for refunds within the `GatewayTransferNative` contract.

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice intends to send 1 BTC to her designated receiver address on the Bitcoin network via `GatewayTransferNative.withdrawToNativeChain` -> `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw`
2. The total cost would amount to 0.9995 BTC plus 0.0005 BTC (for gas fees).
3. The `GatewayTransferNative` contract authorizes 1 BTC + 0.0005 = 1.005 BTC instead of merely 1 BTC (as gas fees are deducted from the output amount).
4. Once Alice's transaction is successful, there will be a remaining allowance of 0.0005 BTC from `GatewayTransferNative` to `GatewayZEVM`.
5. Bob observes this and invokes `GatewayTransferNative.withdraw` to retrieve the 0.0005 BTC allocated for refunds by utilizing the excess allowance.

## Root Cause

Over approval of allowance.

## Impact

Loss of funds for protocol. Insolvency.

## Mitigation

Consider approvving only `outputAmount` in `GatewayTransferNative._handleBitcoinWithdraw`
