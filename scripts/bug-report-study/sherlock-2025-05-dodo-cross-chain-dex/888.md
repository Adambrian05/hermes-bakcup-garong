# Users can exploit the token amounts of others in `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` due to a mismatch between `decoded.targetZRC20` and `zrc20`, enabling them to execute swap and reap profits

## Vulnerability Details 

The `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` function can be triggered via the universal Gateway when a user employs the EVM-compatible `GatewaySend` to deposit assets and selects `GatewayCrossChain` as the target contract. In this process, the user has the ability to define `decoded.targetZRC20`, which is extracted from the `_message` created during the `GatewaySend` call and supplied by the user through `GatewaySend.depositAndCall`. Concurrently, the user will initiate the transfer of a `ZRC20` token, representing the token that `GatewayCrossChain` will receive when the universal Gateway contract calls the `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` function.

The issue arises from the lack of validation to ensure that `decoded.targetZRC20` matches `zrc20`. This oversight permits any attacker to utilize any funds within the `GatewayCrossChain` contract to exchange for gas tokens and keep the remaining amount and forward it to their address in the next EVM compatible chain, thereby profiting and leading to financial losses for the protocol.

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice initiates her attack by transferring 1 USDC (1e6) from Ethereum to Zetachain and then to Polygon.
    - This process involves calling `GatewaySend.depositAndCall`, which triggers `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` by transferring 1 USDC.ETH from universal Gateway to `GatewayCrossChain`
    - Within the `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` function, she configures the `payload` with decoded.targetZRC20 set to `WBTC.ETH`.
2. She notices that the `GatewayCrossChain` contract contains 1e6 worth of `WBTC.ETH` allocated for refunds.
3. Alice successfully executes the `GatewayCrossChain._handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw` call, exchanging 1e6 WBTC.ETH of funds that belong to other users for refunds. After paying for the gas tokens, she ultimately retrieves the remaining funds through the `GatewayCrossChain.withdrawAndCall` to her receiver address on another EVM-compatible chain (Polygon).
4. In the end, Alice forfeits her 1e6 worth of `USDC.ETH`, but she receives 1e6 minus the `gasFee` in `WBTC.ETH`, putting the protocol at a disadvantage and reaps profit

## Root Cause

Insufficient validation to confirm that the token designated for swaps and intended for transfer to the EVM-compatible contract on the Zeta chain matches the supplied ZRC20 token.

## Impact

Loss of funds for protocol. Insolvency.

## Mitigation

Check `zrc20  = decoded.targetZRC20` in the `GatewayCrossChain.onCall -> GatewayCrossChain._swapAndSendERC20Tokens` flow in the else block before doing the swap for gas tokens
