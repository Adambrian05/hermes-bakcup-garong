# through superWithdraw() owner can move any amount of eth/tokens

### Summary

The superWithdraw() function lets the contract owner move any amount of ETH or tokens from the contract to the EddyTreasurySafe. 

In README, statement is there ` EddyTreasurySafe is only trusted to handle protocol fees` .This mismatch could confuse developers or users about the system’s trust limits, potentially leading to misuse or funds being mishandled.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-dodo-cross-chain-dex/blob/main/omni-chain-contracts/contracts/GatewayCrossChain.sol#L162C1-L168C10

### Attack Path

owner calls superWithdraw(token, amount) with an amount that includes user funds or other balances, not just fees.
This breaks the expectation that EddyTreasurySafe only handles protocol fees from users pov.

### Impact

There is a risk of funds being misappropriated by allowing the transfer of the entire contract balance instead of just fees.
