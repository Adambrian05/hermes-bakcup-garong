# Incorrect Token Approval Amount Causes Over-Approval and Potential Failures for Tokens like USDT

### Summary

The _handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw function approves outputAmount + gasFee tokens to the gateway contract, while outputAmount already includes the gas fee. This results in over-approval, which can cause issues especially with tokens like USDT that require allowance to be set to zero before updating. Leftover approvals can lead to failed transactions or unexpected behavior.

### Root Cause

The contract approves an amount equal to outputAmount + gasFee for token transfers, although outputAmount already encompasses the gasFee. Consequently, the approved allowance exceeds the actual amount needed for withdrawal, causing a non-zero leftover allowance.

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-dodo-cross-chain-dex/blob/main/omni-chain-contracts/contracts/GatewayCrossChain.sol#L389

### Attack Path

1. Contract approves more tokens than necessary due to addition of gas fee on top of outputAmount.

2. Leftover approval remains non-zero in the token contract.

3. Subsequent approval attempts or transfers may fail due to non-zero allowance constraints in tokens like USDT.

4. This can lead to failed withdrawals, stuck funds, or an exploitable approval state if a malicious actor gains access.

### Impact

Failed token transfer or withdrawal transactions due to improper allowance management.

Potential denial of service in the withdrawal flow.

### Mitigation

Approve exactly the required amount, i.e., outputAmount without adding gasFee again.
