# Users would lose ETH if the amount transferred is greater than amount

## Summary
When the user tries to execute `depositAndCall` it handles both scenarios of ETH deposits and Erc20 deposits in which the Eth is transferred by the msg.value , However if the user transfer more than the amount , he would lose the extra amount transferred due to lack of refund and insufficient validation.

As we can see it allows for msg.value be greater than amount but the data updated is the amount not the msg.value leading to loss of eth for the user.

Lets say a user tries to make a cross chain call using ETH his msg.value is 10Eth and amount is 1 rather than the call reverting it would execute the function and update user deposit with 1 rather than the 10Eth


## Impact
Lose of eth without refund

## Recommended Mitigation
Enforce msg.value = amount
