# `_handleFeeTransfer()` is too low of a decimal, so that problems may arise (same issue in slippage calculation)

### Summary

Both [_handleFeeTransfer()](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-dodo-cross-chain-dex/blob/main/omni-chain-contracts/contracts/GatewayCrossChain.sol#L297-L300) and slippage calculation -> `uint amountInMax = (amountsQuote[0]) + (slippage * amountsQuote[0]) / 1000` have the issue in which the low decimal precision for fee/slippage calculation will surely produce rounding errors in a token like wbtc.

Given that 1e8 of wbtc is $100k, max amount to round down - (5e3 - 1) is roughly five dollars.

This is in just one cross chain request - a plausible scenario is that a user knowing this, may initiate requests such as `base - arbitrum` or `base - polygon` with such amount of wbtc (like 5e3 - 1), so that he will escape the fee that is to be paid to the `EddyTreasurySafe`.

If he wants to transfer $10k dollars, he will have to pay 0.5% fee, which will be $50 - he can just send small enough txs that will round down and save him $50 in fees in exchange for negligible gas fees on the layer twos!


### Root Cause

The root cause is low decimal precision in calculating fees that will impact tokens like wbtc
