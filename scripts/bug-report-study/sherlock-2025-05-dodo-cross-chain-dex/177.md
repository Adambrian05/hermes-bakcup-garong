# Platform fees are not deducted from the amount prior to executing the swap in `GatewayTransferNative.onCall`

## Vulnerability Details

The protocol facilitates the following process: `(EVM-chain) GatewaySend.depositAndCall` -> `(Zetachain) GatewayTransferNative.onCall`, by designating the `GatewayTransferNative` contract as the target contract within the EVM `GatewaySend` contract during a deposit and call, which subsequently calls the `GatewayTransferNative` contract on Zetachain via `onCall`.

When a user intends to exchange their `targetZRC20` tokens for a different ZRC20 token, the protocol applies an equivalent fee to the amount of ZRC20 tokens being transferred from the gateway to the `GatewayTransferNative` contract before the final token is sent to the user.

The issue arises when these fees are not subtracted from the total amount prior to initiating the swap. As a result, the user may receive more tokens than intended, potentially leading to a loss of funds for the protocol and risking insolvency.

In `GatewayTransferNative.onCall`

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice initiates a call to `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` to exchange 100 USDC on Ethereum for 100 USDT ZRC20 on Zetachain, designating `GatewayTransferNative` as the target contract.
2. During this process, the protocol imposes a 5% fee, amounting to 5 USDC, on her 100 USDC ZRC20 on Zetachain.
3. However, within `GatewayTransferNative`, the amount used for the swap does not account for the fees, and Alice's swap still utilizes the full 100 USDC ZRC20.
4. Consequently, she ends up receiving 100 USDT ZRC20 in return, while the protocol collects 5 USDC ZRC20 as fees, despite her only providing 100 USDC ZRC20 worth of tokens.

## Root Cause

The amount is not reduced by fees prior to the swap in `GatewayTransferNative.onCall`

## Impact

Protocol insolvency, loss of funds.

## Mitigation

Reduce the amount by the platform fees prior to executing the swap through `GatewayTransferNative._doMixSwap`
