# Lack of Protection in `_doMix Swap()` Leads to Front-Running Exploit

## Summary 

The `_doMixswap` function in the `GatewayCrossChain`, `GatewaySend`, and `GatewayTransferNative` contracts is missing the `minReturnAmount` validation that is supposed to be used for protection. This leaves users interacting with the contract vulnerable to slippage without a protection mechanism against malicious actors attempting to manipulate it through front-running attacks.

## Proof of Concept (PoC)

In the PoC, we simulate a frontrunning attack using the sandwich method

Our complete Proof of Concept is saved with the filename `omni-chain-contracts/test/RealContractFrontRunningPOC.t.sol`. The main test is `test_FrontRunningWithRealContract()`.

