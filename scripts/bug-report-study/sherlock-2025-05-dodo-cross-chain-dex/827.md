# Fee-On-Transfer Tokens Cause Revert in depositAndCall Due to Incorrect Assumption on Received Amount

### Summary

In the `GatewaySend` contract, the `depositAndCall`  function assumes that the full amount passed to `transferFrom` will be received by the contract. However, supported tokens like `USDT.ETH` charge a fee on transfer, meaning the actual amount received is less than expected. This discrepancy leads to incorrect downstream assumptions, particularly in `_handleERC20Deposit`, where the function expects the full `amount`, resulting in a revert.

### Root Cause

The contract does **not verify the actual token balance received** after calling `transferFrom`. Instead, it assumes that the exact `amount `was transferred.` Fee-on-transfer` tokens subtract a fee during the transfer, causing the contract to receive less than `amount`. This breaks the logic in subsequent steps that rely on using the full `amount`, particularly when transferring or accounting for it again.

### Impact

Supported tokens like `USDT.ETH `will always revert due to balance mismatch making these token unusable.

### Mitigation

Check the **actual amount received** after `transferFrom` and use that value instead of the user-supplied `amount `in subsequent logic.
