Clumsy Raisin Viper

Medium

# wrong slippage check in _swapAndSendERC20Tokens

### Summary

the amountInMax used in _swapAndSendERC20Tokens is gotten from current onchain price, this is an issue because it assumes whatever price is currently on uniswap is the correct price, which means that if thhe current price does not favor the user then the codebase wouldnt revert the tx 
hence a user can mev the tx by sandwiching the call with a price change on uniswap 

### Root Cause

use of onchain calculated slippage 

### Internal Pre-conditions

none 

### External Pre-conditions

none

### Attack Path

1. alice wants to withdraw some 1000 usdc(its zetachain receipt ) to ethereum 
2. the gas token is zeta hence a swap is needed 
3. the pool used is the zeta/usdc pool (assuming current price = 1 zeta = 0.5usdc) and total amount in gas needed = 4 zeta = 2 usdc 
4. just before alice call, bob makes a swap that moves the current price to 1 zeta = 100 usdc 
5. as a result the onchain amountIn for 4 zeta is calculated as 400 usdc and accounting for amountInMax 410 usdc 
6. the 400 usdc and swapped to 4 zeta and the tx completes 
7. bob completes the mev by backrunning the call and return price to its orginal while gaining ~400 usdc 
8. while alice is left with a ~396 usdc loss 

### Impact

permanent loss of funds
