# Smart wallets cannot receive native tokens via `GatewaySend.onCall`

## Vulnerability Details

The use of `transfer` instead of a low-level `call` in `GatewaySend.onCall` results in transactions reverting for transfers that require more than 2300 gas. For instance, smart wallets can easily surpass this gas limit.

As a consequence, users will inevitably incur losses in gas fees and platform fees when attempting to transfer native ETH that exceeds the 2300 gas threshold from their Zetachain calls via `GatewayTransferNative` or `GatewayCrossChain`.

## Root Cause

Use of transfer with fix gas limit instead of low level call

## Impact

Loss of funds for user.
Broken core functionality.

## Mitigation

Use low level `call` instead of `transfer`
