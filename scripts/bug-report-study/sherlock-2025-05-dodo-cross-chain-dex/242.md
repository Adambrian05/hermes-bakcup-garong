# Inconsistent and Missing Administrative Functions

### Summary

`GatewayCrossChain`, `GatewaySend`, and `GatewayTransferNative` do not have consistency in their administrative functions (setters and withdraws). This could lead to the inability to change key settings or stuck funds in the contract.

The fact that certain contracts have setters and withdraws while others don't is a sign this is an oversight rather than a design choice.

Specifically:

`GatewayCrossChain` is missing `setGasLimit` and `setSlippage`

`GatewaySend` is missing `superWithdraw` and `setDodoApprove`

`GatewayTransferNative` is missing `setSlippage`


### Root Cause

In `GatewayCrossChain`, `setGasLimit` and `setSlippage` are not implemented

In `GatewaySend`, `superWithdraw` and `setDodoApprove` are not implemented

In `GatewayTrasnferNative`, `setSlippage` is not implemented
