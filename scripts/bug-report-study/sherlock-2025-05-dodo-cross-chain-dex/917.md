# Incorrect wallet address utilized when a call is reverted/aborted from Zetachain to Destination Chain (`GatewayCrossChain` -> `GatewaySend`)

## Vulnerability Details

In the `GatewayCrossChain` contract, when a user calls `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` (chain A) -> `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` (Zetachain) -> `GatewayCrossChain.withdrawAndCall`(Zetachain) -> `GatewaySend.onCall` (chain B) and designates `GatewaySend` (chain A) as the `targetContract`, if the transaction fails on the ZetaChain, the sequence of events may unfold as follows: ZetaChain → Ethereum onCall fails → ZetaChain onRevert is triggered. In this case, the `GatewayCrossChain` will also serve as the address for the `revertAddress` (i.e., `GatewayCrossChain`).

If this situation occurs, the `GatewayCrossChain.onRevert` function will be invoked. While the flow is correct, the problem arises when the `GatewayCrossChain` contract includes the destination chain address of the caller in the `RevertOptions.revertMessage`. This leads to the funds becoming trapped in `GatewayCrossChain`, as the Ethereum address should not be utilized; instead, the address owned by the user on ZetaChain should be utilized.

A similar issue may also occur in the `GatewayCrossChain` contract.

## POC

Consider the following scenario

1. Alice invokes `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` (chain A) -> `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` (Zetachain) -> `GatewayCrossChain.withdrawAndCall`(Zetachain) -> `GatewaySend.onCall` (chain B), designating the `GatewaySend` on Ethereum (chain A) contract as the target contract 
    - Entering `GatewayCrossChain.withdrawAndCall`, it includes the `RevertOptions.revertMessage` formatted as `bytes.concat(externalId, bytes20(0xEthereumAlice))`
2. Her call to `GatewayCrossChain.onCall` results in a revert
3. This triggers the `GatewayCrossChain` to revert to the `onRevert` function 
    - In `onRevert`, the revertMessage generated in `GatewaySend.depositAndCall` (chain A) is forwarded to the `onRevert` function
    - The wallet address extracted is `0xEthereumAlice`, rather than the address corresponding to the user's address on `Zetachain`
    - Consequently, her `refundInfos[externalId]` will store `0xAliceEthereum` as her `walletAddress`
4. When Alice attempts to claim her token refund through `GatewayCrossChain.claimRefund`, she is unable to do so, as she may not possess the `0xEthereumAlice` address on Zetachain

## Root Cause

The address provided  `GatewayCrossChain.withdrawAndCall` is wrong on Zetachain in the `RevertOptions.revertMessage` struct, making it incompatible for claiming refunds on Zetachain.

## Impact

Loss of funds for user.

## Mitigation

Use the user's zetachain address instead of their source chain address
