# Missing approval for `gateway` to spend `gasfee` and `amountsOut` in `_handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw()`

### Summary

In the `_handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw()` function, the gas fee can either be the `decoded.targetZRC20` or not and in case it is not, it is swapped to the gas token of the. destination and the gateway withdrawal is called.

### Root Cause

As shown below, if `decoded.targetZRC20 != gasZRC20` then, `_swapAndSendERC20Tokens()` is called to swap the fee into the destination gasfe token

### Attack Path

### Impact

`_handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw()` will revert when `decoded.targetZRC20 == gasZRC20` due to a missing approval thus leading to a DOS

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Approve the gateway to spend the gas fee and amount to be transferred when `decoded.targetZRC20 == gasZRC20`
