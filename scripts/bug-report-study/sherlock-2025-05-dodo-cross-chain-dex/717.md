Special Blue Sardine

Medium

# single gasLimit for SOL and EVMs will cause issues

### Summary

In the `_handleEvmOrSolanaWithdraw` function of `GatewayTransferNative` and `GatewayCrossChain`, a single `gasLimit` is being reused for both EVM and Solana withdrawals.

The problem is that the gas consumption is working differently in both chains, and the gasLimit can be enough for Sol and insufficient in EVMs or vice versa. This will force the admin to set the maximum allowed `gasLimit` globally and force the users to overpay for bridging in all but the most expensive chain (mainnet, for example).

Furthermore `GAS_LIMIT` of [ZRC20](https://github.com/zeta-chain/protocol-contracts/blob/v13.1.0-rc1/contracts/zevm/ZRC20.sol#L334C1-L337C6)s can be updated:

[ZRC20](https://github.com/zeta-chain/protocol-contracts/blob/v13.1.0-rc1/contracts/zevm/ZRC20.sol#L334C1-L337C6)

```solidity
  function updateGasLimit(uint256 gasLimit_) external onlyFungible {
      GAS_LIMIT = gasLimit_;
      emit UpdatedGasLimit(GAS_LIMIT);
  }
```

### Root Cause

Single `gasLimit` property for both EVMs and Sol.

### Internal Pre-conditions

ZRC20s with different `GAS_LIMIT` (can be verified in the respective ZRC20 token’s contracts).

```solidity
USDC.ETH = 100000
USDC.SOL = 100000
USDC.ARB = 400000
```

### External Pre-conditions

None

### Attack Path

Let’s suppose `gasLimit` = 400k to satisfy Arbitrum. Then, users who bridge USDC to Arb pay cents of `gasFee`, and their transactions are executed. On the other hand, users who bridge USDC to Mainnet will pay 4 times the more expensive fee than the max gas limit, which is exposed from the `USDC.ETH`.

### Impact

Overpaying for gas, beyond the enforced `GAS_LIMIT` of the chain’s ZRC20 token.

### PoC

N/A

### Mitigation

Introduce dynamic `gasLimit` mapping, which can be set per destination chain. LayerZero has such a mechanism by `cID`, where they constantly update the fees the users have to pay for each chain separately.
