# ETH Transfer Failure in `onCall`

__Summary:__ The use of the gas-limited `transfer` method for sending ETH to the recipient in `onCall` can cause transactions to fail when the recipient is a contract with a complex receive function.

__Vulnerability Detail:__ In the `onCall` function, when the target token (`toToken`) is ETH, the contract sends the received amount to the recipient address (`evmWalletAddress`) using `payable(evmWalletAddress).transfer(outputAmount);`. The `transfer` method is hardcoded with a gas limit of 2300. If the `evmWalletAddress` is a contract address and its `receive()` or `fallback()` function requires more than 2300 gas to execute (e.g., due to logging, state updates, or internal calls), the `transfer` call will fail.

__Impact:__ Legitimate cross-chain ETH receive operations will fail if the user specifies a recipient contract address whose ETH receiving logic consumes more than 2300 gas. This constitutes a denial-of-service for such users, preventing them from receiving their cross-chain ETH.

__Code Snippet:__
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-dodo-cross-chain-dex/blob/main/omni-chain-contracts/contracts/GatewaySend.sol#L370

__Recommendation:__ Replace the `transfer` call with a low-level `call` and explicitly check its success. This provides more gas to the recipient contract's receive function, allowing it to execute more complex logic.
