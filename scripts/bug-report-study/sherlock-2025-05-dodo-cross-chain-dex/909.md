# Malicious user can bypass fees by breaking their deposits into smaler units.

### Summary

`GatewaySend::depositAndCall` does not charge any fees when users want to perform cross chain transactions to ZEVM. Instead, the fees are charged on the destination chain (Zeta chain). when the relayer calls `GatewayCrossChain::onCall`. 

Also there is no minimum token amount requirement when bridging. A user can bridge any non zero amount on `GatewaySend`. This can be exploited by a malicious user such that they break their deposits into tiny little bits to leverage on roundings and bypass protocol fees. 

Since the protocol will be deployed on L2s with very little gas cost, this is a feasible attack.
