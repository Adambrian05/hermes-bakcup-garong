# Attackers can frontrun auctions to benefit from collateral being sold

### Summary

When liquidations occur, the price of yBOLD lowers because its estimatedTotalAssets includes only the current balance of the contract and the balance in Liquity. Users can buy in after a major liquidation at a low price, wait for an auction to occur, then immediately withdraw at a higher price. They'll be stealing funds owed to other users by doing this. This happens because there are no drip mechanisms for auction revenue and no delays on deposits and withdrawals.

### Root Cause

This is caused by the lack of a drip mechanism such as what `profitMaxUnlockTime` is used for as mentioned here: https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-yearn-ybold/blob/main/README.md?plain=1#L105



### Internal Pre-conditions

N/A

### External Pre-conditions

1. Auction occurs

### Attack Path

1. Deposit BOLD into yBOLD directly before an auction while price is low
2. Wait for the collateral to be sold
3. Withdraw after BOLD has been put back into the vault by the auction

### Impact

Attacker ends up getting instant profit while the other users in the vault who held through the liquidation are repaid less.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Add a drip mechanism for revenue from auctions. It could also be fixed by adding delays on deposits.
