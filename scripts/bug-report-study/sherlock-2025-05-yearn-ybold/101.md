# The auction prices collateral in USD instead of BOLD, causing depositor loss in some situations

### Summary

The strategy uses an auction to swap the `COLL` (Liquity collateral- wstETH, rETH or WETH), into BOLD.

However, when setting the starting price of the auction, the collateral/usd price is used, instead of the collateral/BOLD price.

This would lead to the auction start price being incorrectly calculated, potentially selling collateral at a discount, receiving not enough BOLD to recoup the depositor's assets, causing a loss to depositors.


### Root Cause

In the [`_tend()`](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-yearn-ybold/blob/88a3b89c05fd6e59ec130c79659195cd05d872af/yv3-liquityv2-sp-strategy/src/Strategy.sol#L241) function:

The collateral/usd price is used when setting the auction sell price of the collateral, instead of using the collateral/bold price



### Internal Pre-conditions

N/A

### External Pre-conditions

1. BOLD price is temporarily depegged to be worth less than 1 USD

### Attack Path

1. A dutch auction is started, to sell 1 WETH of collateral into BOLD
- WETH price in USD: $2500
- BOLD price: $0.9
- bufferPercentage=1.1e18

- auction starting price of WETH (in BOLD): 2500 * 1.1e18 = 2750 BOLD per WETH

However, 2750 BOLD is only worth 2750 * 0.9 = $2475 USD

As a result, the auction will be immediately settled since the starting price is already favourable for takers, the strategy effectively sells $2500 worth of WETH, receiving $2475 worth of BOLD. The loss will eventually be reported, causing the share value of yBOLD to go down, causing depositors to lose money.

### Impact
The start price of the dutch auction will be lower than 'bufferPercentage * collateral price', potentially leading to the collateral being sold at a loss.

The depositors to yBOLD and the underlying strategy will experience a loss, due to the value of their shares decreasing.

### Mitigation

Consider incorporating the price oracle for [BOLD/USD](https://www.pyth.network/price-feeds/crypto-bold-usd?range=1M) as this would ensure that the auction start price is correct
