# Flash-loan inflation/deflation cycles around harvests

### Summary

An attacker can use a flash-loan to deposit an arbitrarily large amount of BOLD into the vault just before a keeper harvest when `estimatedTotalAssets()` under-reports because it ignores pending collateral, and then withdraw immediately after the harvest at a higher PPS, pocketing the difference.

### Root Cause

- Unlimited deposits once `openDeposits` is enabled.
- `estimatedTotalAssets()` omits pending collateral gains, so share-price is artificially low just before harvest

### Internal Pre-conditions

`openDeposits == true` (no per-address or total deposit limit).

### External Pre-conditions

- There must be pending collateral to claim (so PPS is depressed).
- A scheduled keeper harvest (or any call to `_harvestAndReport()`) is imminent.

### Attack Path

1. Flash-borrow a large amount of BOLD.
2. Deposit it into the vault immediately before the keeper’s harvest, at the depressed PPS.
3. Let the keeper harvest, which claims the collateral reward, restores PPS upward, and reports gains.
4. Redeem your newly-minted shares at the higher PPS, repaying the flash-loan and pocketing the excess BOLD.

### Impact

Unlimited one-transaction drain of vault yield; no oracle or privileged key control needed; attack is repeatable and only constrained by flash-liquidity and gas.
