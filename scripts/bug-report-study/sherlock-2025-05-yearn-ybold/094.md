# Theft of auctioned collateral by using flash loans to take over the vault

### Summary

Collateral that's being auctioned is able to be stolen by an attacker using flash loans. An attacker can deposit a large amount of BOLD into the yBOLD strategy, win the auction to get the collateral (and in the process recoup almost all of what they spent because they're the largest yBOLD depositor), then withdraw the deposited funds from yBOLD.

This happens because there are no drip mechanisms for users to get their funds replenished slowly and no deposit or withdraw delays.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-yearn-ybold/blob/main/README.md?plain=1#L105

The root cause is that profitMaxUnlockTime is not used and the strategy does not have any similar drip mechanisms. Because of this, the yBOLD vault is instantly replenished so an attacker can deposit, win an auction, and withdraw everything all in one block. There are also no other safeguards such as delays on deposits and withdrawals.

### Internal Pre-conditions

N/A

### External Pre-conditions

1. An auction occurs

### Attack Path

1. Auction begins
2. Attacker flash loans very large amount of BOLD (or collateral that can then borrow BOLD)
3. Attacker mints very large amount of yBOLD (e.g. 100x current TVL) using the loaned BOLD
4. Attacker purchases auction
5. Attacker redeems all yBOLD
6. Attacker repays flash loan and keeps the majority of the collateral

### Impact

The attacker effectively steals all of the collateral at auction. They'll lose a small amount depending on what % of the strategy supply they don't hold--for example if they deposit 99x the current TVL they'll lose 1% of the proceedings of the auction--but overall it will largely be profit. This would happen at every auction.

This does not only result in a loss of yield for users, but that yield came at the expense of users' underlying funds (underlying BOLD is used to pay for the collateral to auction) so it's also a loss of underlying funds for the users.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

BOLD that replenishes the yBOLD vault can be dripped in with `profitMaxUnlockTime` or a similar drip mechanism, which would at least stop this from being able to be flash loaned.

Deposit or withdrawal delays can also be introduced.
