# Fees can be charged by the accountant if PPS is lower than 1

### Summary

The invariant `If process_report reports a loss, Accountant.sol will not take any fees until the yBOLD price per share returns to 1.` does not hold.

### Root Cause

The mechanism of fee charging in `Accountant.sol` is charging a fee regardless of the state of PPS, only accounting for whether there was a gain relative to the last report or not. This is directly breaking the following invariant:
```markdown
If process_report reports a loss, Accountant.sol will not take any fees until the yBOLD price per share returns to 1.
```

### Internal Pre-conditions

None

### External Pre-conditions

1. The system must experience a loss, and subsequently a gain.

### Attack Path

1. Let's assume an user deposits 1000 BOLD in the vault, minting 1000 yBOLD and the vault allocates it to strategy A, leaving current_debt = total_debt = 500. Performance fee is 20%
2. Strategy A suffers a loss of 20 BOLD. It reports that total_assets is 480 but current_debt is 500. In this specific case, because total_locked_shares is 0, to_burn becomes 0. Price Per Share is now _total_assets() / _total_supply() = 0.98. Assume the management fee is zero for simplicity (though this also breaks the invariant).
3. Next period, there's a gain of 10 BOLD. process_report will call the Accountant's report function. No checks are done to verify whether the PPS is above 1 in the accountant contract, so it returns a performance fee. In this case, the fee will be 2.
4. Later on the function, total_fees_shares will be calculated as 2 / 0.98 rounded up, which will be 3, and shares_to_lock will be calculated as the gain, which is 10.
6. At the end of the operation, PPS is (total assets)/(total supply + fees - locked shares), 990/1003 = 0.987. The price is still negative, and a fee was successfully charged.

### Impact

The accountant receives a fee on a negative PPS, breaking an invariant.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Set the skipHealthCheck as true in case the price per share is negative.
