# An attacker will cause potential asset loss for depositors due to incorrect auction pricing

### Summary

The incorrect calculation of the auction starting price in the _tend function will cause potential asset loss for depositors as an attacker will buy collateral at a significantly reduced price.

### Root Cause

In LiquityV2SPStrategy.sol, in the [_tend](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-05-yearn-ybold/blob/main/yv3-liquityv2-sp-strategy/src/Strategy.sol#L225) function, the auction starting price is incorrectly calculated with an extra division by WAD, leading to a significantly lower price than intended.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The strategy must have collateral gains to claim from the Stability Pool.
A keeper or any actor must call the _tend function to initiate the auction.



### External Pre-conditions

None.

### Attack Path

The strategy accumulates collateral gains from the Stability Pool.

A keeper calls the _tend function, which claims the collateral and starts an auction with an incorrectly low starting price.

An attacker monitors the contract and immediately buys the collateral at the low starting price before it decreases further.

The attacker acquires the collateral for a negligible amount of BOLD, while the strategy receives almost no BOLD in return.

### Impact

The depositors suffer an approximate loss of the collateral’s full value as it is sold for a fraction of its worth. The attacker gains the collateral by paying a negligible amount of BOLD.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Correct the starting price calculation in the _tend function to:
AUCTION.setStartingPrice((_available * _price * _bufferPercentage) / (WAD * WAD));
