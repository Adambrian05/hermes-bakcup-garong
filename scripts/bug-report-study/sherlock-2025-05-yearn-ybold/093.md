# Auctions can be forced to always take their full period (not kick DOS), resulting in higher likelihood of "bad debt"

### Summary

The strategy contract enforces a minimum dust threshold to ensure it doesn't try to start auctions for tiny amounts, but the Auction contract does not enforce any such minimum when attempting to `settle` an auction. As long as there is 1 wei of collateral within the auction contract the auction must continue, and no one will bid on an auction for such a small amount.

During volatile price movements (crashes) attackers can ensure there's always 1 wei in the auction contract so that every auction must take the maximum amount of time. This can lead to increased risk of collateral being auctioned off for less than the BOLD users paid for it.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy-periphery/blob/8442563a8d2b7a71057c76bdcead9b346927c301/src/Auctions/Auction.sol#L608

This line shows where an auction cannot be settled and a new one started until the contract is completely empty. This is fine for most Yearn auctions because they are less time sensitive, but it's extremely important for tend and auction to occur as soon as possible so that yBOLD holders can regain their losses.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Auction is started

### External Pre-conditions

1. Large downturn in collateral will do the biggest damage

### Attack Path

1. When an auction is concluding with the last buy (or the attacker is the last buyer), attacker then sends 1 wei to the auction contract
2. No one will purchase this auction and it will not be able to be settled until the end of the period


### Impact

The impact depends on the loss of collateral value during a downturn. It's very important for keepers to regularly `tend` to avoid holders being hurt by lowering collateral prices, so delays in auctions while price is dropping and liquidations are occurring could result in significant harm to users.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Add a minimum auction threshold to the settle function so that it can settle an auction if there's only dust left in the contract.
