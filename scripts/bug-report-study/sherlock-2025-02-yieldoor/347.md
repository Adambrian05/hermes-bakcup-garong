# Lack of slippage when borrowing assets

## Summary
The `openLeveragedPosition` function in the contract lacks slippage protection during the borrowing and swapping process. Specifically, when the function borrows the denomination token and swaps it for the required assets, it does not enforce any slippage limits. This omission can lead to significant losses if the price changes unfavorably between transaction execution and swap completion, potentially exposing users to unexpected and costly price impacts

The contract first flashloans funds to open a position and then borrows a specified amount of the denomination token.The borrowed tokens are swapped to provide the necessary liquidity for the leveraged position.
However, the swaps are executed without slippage control, meaning that if market conditions shift, the user may receive significantly fewer tokens than expected.

## Impact
Loss of expected tokens for users
## Recommended Mitigation
Add a slippage protection
