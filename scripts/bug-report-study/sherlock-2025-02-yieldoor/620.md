# First Depositor Attack on Vault Manipulates Token Ratio, Forcing Users to Buy at Higher Prices

### Summary

In the Vault contract, the ratio of token0 and token1 is determined by the first depositor and changes dynamically based on Uniswap swaps. An attacker can manipulate this ratio by being the first depositor and forcing future users to buy token0 at an inflated price.

Attack scenario:
	1.	The attacker holds a large amount of token0 and deposits 1 token1 with an excessive amount of token0.
	2.	This creates a severely unbalanced ratio, discouraging users from depositing normally.
	3.	Any new participant must purchase token0 to match the vault ratio, causing token0’s price to rise.
	4.	The attacker sells token0 at a higher price for profit while still holding protocol shares, benefiting from any future token donations.

This forces users to buy token0 at an inflated price, making the protocol unfairly expensive for new participants.


### Root Cause

In [Vault.sol](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L119-L151), the first depositor determines the initial token0/token1 ratio, which can be highly imbalanced if manipulated.

### Internal Pre-conditions

Future deposits must follow the vault’s current ratio, forcing new users to buy token0.

### External Pre-conditions

1.	token0 is traded on a market like Uniswap, where price fluctuations can be exploited.
2.	The attacker has a large amount of token0 before launching the attack.
3.	Other users wish to join the vault, creating demand for token0.

### Attack Path

1.	The attacker deposits 1 token1 and a large amount of token0, setting an extreme ratio.
2.	New users must buy token0 at a high price to deposit into the vault.
3.	The increased demand for token0 drives up its market price.
4.	The attacker sells token0 at a higher price, securing a profit.


### Impact

•	Users are forced to buy token0 at artificially high prices, making entry costly.
•	The attacker profits at the expense of new participants, creating an unfair system.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Restrict the ratio deviation for deposits after the first deposit.
