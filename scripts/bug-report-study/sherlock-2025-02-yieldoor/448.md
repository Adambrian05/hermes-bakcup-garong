# `maxTimesLeverage` does not affected

### Summary
The flawed calculation of the leverage rate allows users to open leveraged positions that exceed the intended limit.

### Root Cause
The `initCollateralValue` represents the total collateral value, which includes the borrowed amount.
Therefore, the calculated `positionLeverage` always less than 2.

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L464

### PoC
Let's assume the following:
maxTimesLeverage = 2, 
Alice's position: actual collateral = $100 , initCollateralValue = $4,000, which represents 40x leverage.

At this moment, `borrowedUsd = 3900 usd`.
In L464, `positionLeverage = (4,000 + 3900) / 4,000 = 1.975 < 2`

### Impact
Users can open leverage positions greater than intended.
While some users may be able to borrow large amounts of assets with little collateral, others may be unable to borrow due to insufficient assets.

### Mitigation
Consider changing the formula or subtracting the borrowed amount from `initCollateralValue`.
