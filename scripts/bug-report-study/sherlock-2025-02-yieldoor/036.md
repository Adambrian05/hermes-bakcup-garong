# Incorrect Protocol Fee Calculation May Lead to Misallocation of Funds

### Summary

The `collectFees` function calculates protocol fees using the formula:

```solidity
uint256 protocolFeeAmount = (afterBal0 - preBal0) * protocolFee / 10_000;
```

This calculation assumes that `afterBal0 >= preBal0`. However, if another function withdraws funds before `collectFees` is called, `afterBal0` might be lower than `preBal0`, leading to:

- Incorrect protocol fee distribution – fees may be negative or miscalculated.
- Unintentional fee deductions – could result in fund loss or protocol receiving incorrect amounts.


### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L172

### Internal Pre-conditions

- The function `collectFees` is executed.
- The contract maintains balances `preBal0` and `afterBal0` for fee calculation.

### External Pre-conditions

- Another function or contract (e.g., a withdrawal or rebalance mechanism) modifies the contract’s balance before `collectFees` completes execution.
- `afterBal0` becomes lower than `preBal0`, violating the expected balance assumption.

### Attack Path

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L159

### Impact

- Incorrect fee collection: The protocol might collect an incorrect amount or even suffer from unintended deductions.
- Potential fund loss: The protocol’s earnings might be negatively affected, reducing expected revenue.

- Anyone can trigger the function at an unintended time – A malicious actor or an external party could call `collectFees` immediately after a withdrawal but before new fees have been collected, causing incorrect calculations.

Add checks to ensure `afterBal0 >= preBal0` before fee computation.
