# Bad debt positions may remain in leverager because of ineffective liquidation logic design for liquidators

### Summary

Bad debt positions may remain in leverager because of ineffective liquidation logic design for liquidators

### Root Cause

In [Strategy::liquidatePosition()](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L299) function, if there is a bad debt occured because of price fluctations, liquidator should pay the debt and get collateral amount from the position.

In current design, liquidations can be executed only fully and protocol does not support partial liquidations. Let say a position will be liquidated. Liquidator should pay whole debt no matter collateral value and he can get the funds from position.

The problem is bad debt liquidations are not incentivized by the protocol. Liquidators won't repay the debt amount for bad debt positions due to mishandling bad debt scenario.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Bad debt is occured

### External Pre-conditions

None

### Attack Path

No need an attack path, just a position should be in bad debt. No body is incentivized to liquidate such a position.

### Impact

There is different kind of handlings for other lending platforms. For instance, prisma distribute the bad debt to whole suppliers and it becomes negligible due to high amount of suppliers or AAVE is always incetivize the users to liquidate such a positions and then they handle the bad debt by Umbrella system. 

The current system design of Yieldoor does not incentivize the liquidators to liquidate bad debt position and this makes liquidation is delayed and it may cause more bad debt due to delay. Most probably, only protocol itself will liquidate such a position. In the end, if no body liquidates the position, it will cause loss of funds for the last withdrawer in lending supply pool.


### Mitigation

I really like the way Prisma used. Token indexes can be adjusted and the bad debt can be distributed to the whole supplier lending pool.
