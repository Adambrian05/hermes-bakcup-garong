### Summary

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Strategy contract must use TWAP as primary price feed for rebalancing
2. TWAP observation window must be ≤10 minutes (per docs)
3. No spot-TWAP divergence check exists in _requirePriceWithinRange()
4. Liquidity positions must have ≥$1M TVL to make attack profitable
5. Attacker needs access to flash loans (>$5M)

### External Pre-conditions

Uniswap pool has sufficient liquidity for large swaps

### Attack Path

1. Attacker borrows $5M USDC via flash loan
2. Executes massive swap (USDC→WETH) to skew TWAP by 15%
3. Calls rebalance() triggering liquidity removal/redeployment at manipulated TWAP price
4. Reverses swap (WETH→USDC) after rebalance completes
5. Price returns to original level, leaving LP positions concentrated outside active range

### Impact

- Loss of LP funds due to bad liquidity positioning
- Significant impermanent loss (up to 30% of LP funds).

### Mitigation

Add spot-TWAP divergence check:
```solidity
function _requirePriceWithinRange() internal view {  
    (, int24 currentTick,,,,) = pool.slot0();  
    int24 twapTick = twapTick();  
    require(abs(currentTick - twapTick) < maxTickDivergence, "TWAP manipulated");  
}
```
