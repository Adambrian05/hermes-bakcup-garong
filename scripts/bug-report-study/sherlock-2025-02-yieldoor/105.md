# Deposit fee bypass in Vault#deposit()

### Summary

The depositFee is expected to be capped at 0.1% (value of 10 in basis points), meaning users should pay a small portion of their shares as a fee.
This implies that if attackers repeatedly deposit and withdraw, the owner may set a nonzero depositFee to discourage this behavior. However, attacker can avoid fees to receive shares.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L61
Solidity rounds down integer division, meaning tiny deposits may avoid paying any fee due to shares * depositFee / 10_000 rounding to zero.
Thus spam user can avoid this fee with small deposit amount and normal user can suffer from this fee.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The deposit function accepts any deposit size.
The deposit fee calculation rounds down small amounts to zero.

### External Pre-conditions

The attacker has enough gas and funds to spam deposits and withdrawals.
The attacker repeats the process with small amounts.

### Attack Path

Attacker deposits a very small amount of token0 and token1.
The contract calculates shares using _calcDeposit().
Since the deposit is small, shares * depositFee / 10_000 rounds down to zero.
Attacker gets shares without paying a fee.
The attacker repeats this process thousands of times to accumulate shares for free.
The attacker later withdraws the full amount, profiting unfairly.

### Impact

Attackers can avoid the intended deposit fee, creating unfair share distribution.

_No response_

### Mitigation

Enforce a minimalThreshold for deposit.
