# TWAP interval is set too low

### Summary

A short TWAP interval will cause an increased risk of price manipulation for the protocol as an attacker will exploit the Uniswap V3 TWAP.

### Root Cause

In the constructor of Strategy.sol the TWAP interval is set to 300 seconds (5 minutes) [here](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L100), which is lower than the typical 1,800 seconds (30 minutes) used by Uniswap V3. This choice to use a shorter TWAP interval is a mistake as it increases the protocol's vulnerability to price manipulation, especially when the liquidity of the Uniswap pool is not spread over a wide range.

### Internal Pre-conditions

Pool should not be a stable coin pool for this attack to work.

### External Pre-conditions

none

### Attack Path

**1st Attack Path**

The protocol uses a single price feed based on Uniswap V3 TWAP oracle.

However, in the constructor of Strategy.sol a TWAP of 300 secs (5mins), which is lower than typical 1,800 secs (30mins) that is used by  UniswapV3 in their studies. This puts the protocol at a higher risk of price manipulation when liquidity of the  pool is not spread over a wide range. This could occur during protocol launch where the Uniswap pool has limited liquidity.

One may argue that such price manipulation is risky for the attacker, as the attacker has to use their own capital (instead of flash loan) to keep the price manipulated for more than a block, making them vulnerable to arbitrage. But that is not a total deterrence as shown in [Rari's Fuse hack](https://cmichel.io/replaying-ethereum-hacks-rari-fuse-vusd-price-manipulation/), where the attacker risked their capital and waited for multiple blocks. The root cause of that hack was due to price manipulation of the Uniswap V3 TWAP oracle, which had a TWAP duration of 600 secs and the Uniswap pool did not have liquidity over a wide range. 
This protocol has a TWAP duration of half than that and the attacker need to only manipulate it by [2%](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L101).

The attacker will manipulate TWAP to cause [_priceWithinRange()](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L298) to return false.
This would thus cause DOS on `rebalance()`, `compund()` and `addVestingPosition()` which call `_priceWithinRange()`, thus making the attack far more easier to carry out.

**2nd Attack Path**
2% is normal deviation which can happen in any pool over a 5 minutes period. Therefore market fluctuation or even simple swap transactions can cause this deviation and force `_priceWithinRange()` to return false. Therefore every big swap on large pool, small swap on small pool or small market fluctuation would make critical functionalities to stop working.

### Impact

This would cause frequent DOS on `rebalance()`, `compund()` and `addVestingPosition()` in Strategy.sol, which are core functionalities of the protocol either by attacker or in scenarios described above.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Increase the TWAP duration to at least 1800, so that it becomes more difficult to carry out the attack. And also increase the `tickTwapDeviation` value.
