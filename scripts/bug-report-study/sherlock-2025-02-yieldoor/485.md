# Liquidator Will Steal Collateral via Price Manipulation for LPs

### Summary

The reliance on manipulatable spot prices for liquidation profit calculation in Leverager.sol will cause collateral theft for liquidity providers (LPs) as a liquidator will temporarily spike spot prices to claim inflated protocol fees

Link code: https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L299-L380

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Liquidation fee must be based on profit derived from spot price
2. No TWAP/oracle check exists during liquidation profit calculation
3. Low liquidity in the underlying pool (≤$1M TVL)
4. Liquidator has access to flash loans (>$100k)

### External Pre-conditions

1. Uniswap pool allows large price impact from small swaps


### Attack Path

1. Liquidator borrows $100k via flash loan
2. Executes small swap (e.g., $10k) to temporarily spike spot price by 15%
3. Calls liquidatePosition() during price spike, claiming 15% "profit"
4. Pays 15% fee to protocol based on fake profit
5. Reverses swap to restore original price

### Impact

- Claims fee (15%) from non-existent profit.
- Theft of collateral via false profit reporting.
