# Lack of Updates Reserve's `UnderlyingBalance`

### Summary

The failure to update the reserve’s underlying balance in the `pushFunds` and `pullFunds` functions will cause an underreporting or overreporting of available liquidity for borrowers and lenders as the leverager will push tokens or pull Tokens back without synchronizing the reserve state.

### Root Cause

In `LendingPool.sol` the `pullFunds` and `pushFunds` function directly transfers underlying tokens by calling `transferUnderlyingTo` without updating `reserve.underlyingBalance`.

### Internal Pre-conditions

vulnerability exist in two functions both of them will have different prerequisites:

pushFunds ->

->The leverager calls pushFunds to return tokens.
->The underlying tokens are transferred, but no state update occurs.

pullFunds ->
->Leverager calls pullFunds to withdraw tokens from the yToken contract.
->The reserve’s underlying balance remains unchanged despite the transfer.

### External Pre-conditions

vulnerability exist in two functions both of them will have different prerequisites:

pushFunds ->
->The pushed funds are not immediately accounted for in external liquidity queries.
->Market participants rely on reserve state for borrow/redeem calculations.

pullFunds ->
->An attacker or misbehaving leverager triggers a pullFunds call.
->Market conditions do not force an immediate state update, allowing the discrepancy to persist.

### Attack Path

The leverager calls pullFunds or pushFunds to transfer tokens.

Since reserve.underlyingBalance is not updated, the contract’s state still shows higher or lower liquidity than available.

Subsequent withdrawals or redeems based on reserve state fail or cause insolvency.

### Impact

->Inaccurate liquidity reporting may lead to underutilization of deposited funds, failed borrow transactions, or incorrect interest calculations, potentially harming protocol participants.

->Lenders may be unable to redeem their yTokens due to insufficient underlying assets, even though the reserve’s state appears to have ample funds. This discrepancy can lead to severe liquidity shortfalls.

### Mitigation

->After transferring tokens in pushFunds, add code to increase reserve.underlyingBalance by the pushed amount.

->Before transferring tokens in pullFunds, update the reserve’s underlying balance to reflect the withdrawal. For example, after transferring, subtract the transferred amount from reserve.underlyingBalance.
