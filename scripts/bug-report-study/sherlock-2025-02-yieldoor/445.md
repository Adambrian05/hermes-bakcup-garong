# There is a lack of state update in the `setBorrowingRateConfig` function.

### Summary
There is a lack of state update in the `LendingPool::setBorrowingRateConfig` function.

### PoC
After setting the borrowing rate, the changed borrowing rates are not applied until updateInterestRates is called. 
When updateState() is called, the old borrowing rates are applied.

### Impact
Borrowers may end up paying more or less interest than they should.
