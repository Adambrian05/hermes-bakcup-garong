# Lack mechanism for position owner to prevent liquidation by increasing collateral or decreasing borrow amount

### Summary

The `Leverager` contract allows position owners to decrease both collateral and borrow amounts proportionally using the `withdraw` function. However, there is no mechanism for position owners to independently increase collateral or decrease the borrow amount to prevent liquidation. This limitation can lead to unnecessary liquidations, even if the position owner is willing to add more collateral or reduce the borrowed amount.

### Root Cause

The [`withdraw`](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L209) function only allows proportional reduction of both collateral and borrowed amounts.
There is no separate function to:

1. Increase collateral without affecting the borrowed amount.
2. Decrease the borrowed amount without affecting the collateral.

### Attack Path

1. A position becomes at risk of liquidation due to market volatility or price changes.
2. The position owner attempts to prevent liquidation by increasing collateral or reducing the borrowed amount.
3. The owner realizes that the only available option is to call `withdraw`, which reduces both collateral and borrowed amounts proportionally.
4. The position remains at risk of liquidation, and the owner cannot take further action to prevent it.

### Impact

Position owners cannot prevent liquidation by increasing collateral or reducing the borrowed amount.
This can lead to unnecessary liquidations, causing financial losses to position owners.
