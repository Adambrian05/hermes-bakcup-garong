# The Amount of Dust Remaining as Debt is Not Negligible

### Summary
The amount of dust remaining as debt is not negligible.

### Root Cause
When calculating the amount of debt to be repaid, the contract rounds down the repayment amount. 
However, the dust remaining as debt is not negligible.

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L221-L222
```solidity
    function withdraw(WithdrawParams calldata wp) external nonReentrant {
        ...
221:    uint256 totalOwedAmount = up.borrowedAmount * bIndex / up.borrowedIndex;
222:    uint256 owedAmount = totalOwedAmount * wp.pctWithdraw / 1e18;
        ...
    }
```

### PoC

> Loan repayments:
> Loan repayments are rounded down. Over time, a few wei could remain as permanent debt. This number should be low enough to not care about it.

However, the collateral is `wbtc`, this is problematic.

Assuming the following:
    block period = 15s, the `withdraw` fucntion is called every block, the dust loss is 1 wei, the wbtcPrice = 80000 usd.
The amount of dust loss for 1 day = 24 * 60 * 60 / 15 = 5760 (4.6 usd)
The amount of dust loss for 1 year = 4.6 * 365 (1,679 usd)

### Impact
Providers in the `LendingPool` may not receive the above amount of assets due to this issue.

### Mitigation
Consider rounding up in the calculations for `totalOwedAmount` and `owedAmount`.
