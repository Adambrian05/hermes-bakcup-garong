# Protocol changing liquidation fee in the Leverager may frontrun liquidator, leading to loss of funds

### Summary

The `Leverager` allows changing the liquidation fee at any time, so liquidators may be frontrunned by this change and get penalized.

### Root Cause

In [Leverager:liquidatePosition()](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L299), the liquidation fee is not checked.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Owner changes liquidation fee.

### External Pre-conditions

None.

### Attack Path

1. Liquidator liquidates a position.
2. Owner frontruns and increases liquidation fee.
3. Liquidator transaction is settled after the owner's.

### Impact

Loss of fees for the liquidator who may have done a bad trade.

### PoC

None.

### Mitigation

Allow the liquidator to check the specific liquidation fee used to check if it is profitable. There is no cap in the liquidation fee and it may even become 100%, taking the liquidator a guaranteed loss.
