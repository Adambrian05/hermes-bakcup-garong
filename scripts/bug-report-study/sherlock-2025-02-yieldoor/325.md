# First depositor can manipulate the share price by providing imbalanced amounts.

in vault.sol:_calcDeposit

The vulnerability arises from an improper calculation of shares when the vault is initialized. Specifically, the first depositor can manipulate the share price by providing imbalanced deposit amounts. This can lead to unfair share distribution and potential value extraction.
The first depositor determines the share price by depositing an arbitrary ratio of tokens. if one token is significantly underrepresented, subsequent depositors will receive fewer shares relative to their deposits. this results in unfair advantage for the first depositor and potential losses for later depositors.

Impact:

-First depositor could manipulate the share price ratio
-Subsequent depositors could receive fewer shares than they should
-Value extraction possible through share price manipulation

Fix:
Require both tokens to be deposited or enforce ratio validation using oracle.
