# Leverager can freely withdraw tokens using pullFunds(...) without updating reserve data

### Summary

The missing update to reserve data in pullFunds(...) will cause a complete loss of funds for the lending protocol as a leverager will repeatedly withdraw tokens without any accounting adjustments.

### Root Cause

In yToken.sol, the function pullFunds(...) transfers tokens from the yToken contract without updating the reserve's underlying balance or the total borrowed amount.

### Internal Pre-conditions

Leverager needs to call pullFunds(...) with any amount.

The LendingPool must contain sufficient liquidity to fulfill the request.

The contract must fail to update reserve.underlyingBalance or totalBorrows.

### External Pre-conditions

No external protocol needs to update the LendingPool’s reserve balances before the attack occurs.

Gas prices remain stable to ensure repeated executions.

### Attack Path

The leverager calls pullFunds(asset, amount), transferring tokens from yToken to their own wallet.

Since pullFunds(...) does not update the reserve balance, the LendingPool remains unaware of the withdrawal.

The protocol continues reporting a higher liquidity than actually available.

The attacker repeats this process until the entire reserve is drained.

### Impact

This vulnerability allows the leverager to withdraw tokens indefinitely, leading to a total depletion of the lending pool's reserves. The protocol's accounting remains unchanged, bypassing all borrowing constraints and interest accrual mechanisms. This results in a high-severity financial loss and potential insolvency of the lending platform.
