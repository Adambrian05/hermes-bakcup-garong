# Significant Loss of Funds Due to Slippage

### Summary
The acceptance of a 2% slippage in protocols is significant and can lead to substantial financial losses.

### Root Cause
The protocol allows for a 2% change in the price when interacting with Uniswap. 
This means that during deposits to Uniswap, a slippage of 2% is tolerated. 
Given the scale of the protocol's funds, this 2% can represent a considerable loss.

```solidity
    function _priceWithinRange() internal view returns (bool) {
        int24 _twapTick = twapTick();
        (, int24 currentTick,,,,,) = IUniswapV3Pool(pool).slot0();

        int24 minTick = _twapTick - tickTwapDeviation;
        int24 maxTick = _twapTick + tickTwapDeviation;
        if (currentTick < minTick || currentTick > maxTick) return false;
        return checkPoolActivity();
    }
```
In a typical scenario, if the positions are out of range, the Rebalancer may call the rebalance function. 
At this point, removing liquidity does not directly relate to the slippage price; however, when adding liquidity, the slippage can result in a 2% loss of funds. 
If the protocol has assets worth 1,000,000 usd, a 2% slippage would equate to a loss of 20,000 usd. 
This amount of funds could be exploited by a validator on the chain to attempt a Sandwich attack.

### Impact
The vault could incur significant losses due to slippage.

### Mitigation
To reduce the risk of slippage-induced losses, consider splitting the funds during rebalancing.
