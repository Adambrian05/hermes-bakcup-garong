# Whale LP providers can increase borrowingIndex in favor of themself


### Root Cause

The utilization rate is calculated as the total borrowed amount divided by the sum of the total reserve and the total borrowed amount. Additionally, the borrowing rate and borrowing index are computed based on the utilization rate, which are two important factors in determining the owed amount. However, large liquidity providers can manipulate the utilization rate in their favor
