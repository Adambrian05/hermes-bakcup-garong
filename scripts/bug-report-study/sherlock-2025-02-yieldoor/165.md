# Lending Pool interest rate keeps growing even when contract is paused

### Summary

The Lending Pool interest rate should stop when the contracts are paused as the borrowers can not withdraw their positions in the leverager.

### Root Cause

Interest [always](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/LendingPool.sol#L272) accrues in the Lending Pool.

### Internal Pre-conditions

None.

### External Pre-conditions

None.

### Attack Path

1. One of the contracts is paused, Lending Pool or Vault.
2. User can not withdraw their position from the leverager and will keep paying interest.

### Impact

User pays interest without being able to close the position.

### PoC

None.

### Mitigation

The index should not be updated when the Lending Pool, or the Vault are paused.
