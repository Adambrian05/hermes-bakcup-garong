# pushFunds(...) Fails to Update LendingPool's underlyingBalance

### Summary

The pushFunds(...) function failing to update underlyingBalance will cause a desynchronization in the LendingPool’s reserve tracking as tokens transferred back to the yToken contract are not reflected in the protocol’s internal accounting, leading to inaccurate interest rate calculations and borrow interactions.

### Root Cause

In pushFunds(...), tokens are transferred back to the yToken contract but reserve.underlyingBalance is not updated in the LendingPool contract, causing a mismatch between the actual and recorded balance.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The leverager calls pushFunds(...) to transfer tokens back to the yToken contract.

The function completes the transfer but does not update reserve.underlyingBalance.

The LendingPool continues using an outdated balance for interest rate calculations and borrowing capacity checks.

### External Pre-conditions

The protocol depends on underlyingBalance for liquidity calculations instead of checking the actual balance.

No external function synchronizes the recorded and actual balances after pushFunds(...) execution.

### Attack Path

The leverager calls pushFunds(...), transferring amount of the underlying asset back to the yToken contract.

The function does not update reserve.underlyingBalance, leaving the internal reserve tracking unchanged.

Borrowers and depositors interact with the protocol based on incorrect utilization rates and reserve levels.

This discrepancy may allow mispriced interest rates, unexpected borrowing conditions, or liquidity mismanagement.

### Impact

This vulnerability leads to inaccurate interest rate calculations and distorted liquidity tracking. Borrowers and depositors may face incorrect borrowing limits, and the LendingPool could make misinformed liquidity management decisions, affecting overall protocol stability.
