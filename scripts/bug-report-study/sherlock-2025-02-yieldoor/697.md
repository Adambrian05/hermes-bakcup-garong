# The withdraw of vesting position with arbitrary amount could break the token0/token1 amount ratio in vault

### Summary

The withdraw of vesting position could break the token0/token1 amount ratio in vault. The shares' matching token0 and token1 amount could change for protocol users. The users could have loss.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L119-L151
The share is minted based on the balances of strategy.
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L157-L161
The vest position's token amount is decided by owner. It could be any amount.
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L159-L181
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L459-L477
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L486-L495
The withdraw of vest position is done by calling `collectFees`. And the withdraw amount is flowing into strategy balances.
So if the vest position's token amount ratio is different from original vault token ratio. The vault token ratio will be changed.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. Owner call `addVestingPosition` with arbitrary input.

### External Pre-conditions

N/A

### Attack Path

1. Owner call `addVestingPosition` with arbitrary input.
2. The vest position is withdrawn by calling `collectFees`.
3. The vault token ratio is changing.

### Impact

The users' share matching token0 and token1 amount is changing. The users maybe have loss.

### PoC

_No response_
