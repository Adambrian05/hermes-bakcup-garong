# The Loss of Interest

### Summary
When the collateral in the LendingPool is WBTC, a loss occurs during the calculation of the interest.

### Root Cause
The decimal precision of WBTC is insufficient for accurately calculating the interest.

### PoC
Assume the following parameters:
    utilizationA := 80%, borrowingRateA := 20%, utilizationRate := 40%, reserve.totalBorrows := 0.2 wbtc
currentBorrowingRate = 40% * 20% / 80% = 10%.
ratePerSecond = 0.1e27 / 365 days ~= 3.17e18
After 1 block (15 seconds), the increased ratio of the index is less than 1e27 + 48e18

The interest accrued for 1 block is less than `2e7 * 48e18 / 1e27 < 1 wei`.
As a result, if the `updateState` function is called every block, the interest will never accumulate.

### Impact
Interest will either never accumulate or accumulate less than expected.

### Mitigation
Consider increasing the decimal precision.
