# Missing Check for totalLiquidity >= totalBorrows in ReserveLogic Library

### Summary

The absence of a check ensuring totalLiquidity is always greater than or equal to totalBorrows will cause an undercollateralized state for the lending protocol as users may be unable to withdraw funds if borrows exceed available liquidity.

### Root Cause

In ReserveLogic.sol, multiple functions such as deposit(), redeem(), borrow(), and repay() modify liquidity and borrow balances but do not enforce a constraint that ensures totalLiquidity >= totalBorrows

### Internal Pre-conditions

A user calls borrow() or repay() without validation checks enforcing totalLiquidity >= totalBorrows.

Interest rate calculations or state inconsistencies cause totalBorrows to exceed available liquidity.

The LendingPool does not perform balance consistency checks across functions that modify reserves.

### External Pre-conditions

Market volatility or rapid borrowing events create conditions where borrows outpace liquidity.

No external mechanism (e.g., liquidation or collateralization enforcement) corrects the imbalance before withdrawals occur.

### Attack Path

A user calls borrow() and increases reserve.totalBorrows without verifying totalLiquidity is sufficient.

Another user attempts to withdraw funds through redeem(), but there is insufficient liquidity due to excessive borrows.

If there are rounding errors or unexpected interest rate fluctuations, the imbalance worsens over time.

The protocol enters an undercollateralized state where users cannot withdraw their deposited funds.

### Impact

This vulnerability can lead to liquidity depletion, making it impossible for users to withdraw their funds. Since the protocol relies on complex interest rate calculations, inconsistencies in reserve tracking may result in insolvency, leading to financial losses and destabilizing the lending platform.
