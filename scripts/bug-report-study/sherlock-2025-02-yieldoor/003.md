# System Lockup Due to Invalid Ticks in Vesting Positions.

### Summary

System Insolvency Due to Invalid Ticks in Vesting Position
The improper handling of vested positions in the Strategy::collectFees function causes most system functionalities to fail, as collectFees may revert with an "NP" error.

### Root Cause

`Strategy.sol:167` - must be collectPositionFees(vestPosition.tickLower, vestPosition.tickUpper);

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. `Owner` needs to call `addVestingPosition` with the specific `_vestDuration`
2. `Rebalancer` needs to call `rebalance`  


### External Pre-conditions

1. After a vesting position is added — and before rebalancing — the pool's price must move outside the boundaries of the `mainPosition`.

### Attack Path

1. `Owner` must call `addVestingPosition` with the specified _vestDuration.
2. After this call — and before both the next step and the vesting position's expiration — the pool’s price must move outside the bounds of the `mainPosition`.
3. Finally, the `Rebalancer` must invoke the `rebalance` function before the vesting position expires.

### Impact

Due to invalid parameters passed on line `Strategy.sol:167` (https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L167), the `Strategy::collectFees()` function reverts with an "NP" error, effectively locking all user funds in the system.

Replace mainPosition.tickUpper with `vestPosition.tickUpper` in the line Strategy.sol:167
