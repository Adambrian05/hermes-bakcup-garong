# Allowing Repayment When Paused  {impact} {affected party}

### Summary

The protocol has a pause mechanism likely to halt operations during emergencies e.g., an exploit, market crash, or liquidity drain.
However, the `repay()` function bypasses the pause restriction, allowing borrowers to continue repaying even when the system is paused While this may seem beneficial it can also create attack vector 

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/LendingPool.sol#L186

### Root Cause

Missing paused mechanism in the repay function 
https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/LendingPool.sol#L186

### Internal Pre-conditions

State inconsistencies 
No Global state synchronization 

### External Pre-conditions

Market crisis
Liquidity shock
Oracle manipulation 

### Attack Path

A critical vulnerability is detected (e.g., oracle manipulation, extreme market volatility, or a liquidity drain).
The protocol pauses to prevent further damage and freezes operation (e.g., deposits, withdrawals, new loans)
Normally, pausing all users in place to stop attackers from exploiting inconsistent state

**Attackers Use Repayment to Manipulate Reserves Balance**
Since repayment is still allowed attackers can partially repay their debt and free up collateral while everyone is still frozen or repay their debt at a very low rate due to market crash 
Attackers rely their debt making them solvent
If liquidations bots are disabled due to the pause, they avoid liquidation while everyone else is vulnerable 
They would be able to withdraw/sell their asset at good rate compare to when they repay their debt at very low rate 

### Impact

- Selective Exploitations of paused condition
- Liquidity manipulation I.e allowing attackers to manipulate interest rate or liquidation threshold 
- potential collateral exploit

_No response_

### Mitigation

Restrict Repayment in pause mode to prevent exploit 

You can Allow only whitelisted Emergency repayment I.e., only trusted actors
