# No incentives for liquidators in `Leverager`

### Summary

In leveraged trading, liquidation bonus (or liquidation incentive) is an additional reward given to liquidators who execute forced liquidations on undercollateralized positions. This bonus is essential for ensuring the efficiency and stability of the trading system.

However, the absence of a liquidation bonus may disincentivize liquidators.

### Root Cause

The [`liquidatePosition()`](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L299-L380) function doesn't provide any benefit to liquidators.

And even worse, the function takes liquidation fee from a liquidation caller.

### Internal pre-conditions

- A position should be liquidatable

### External pre-conditions

*None*

### Attack Path

*None*

### Impact

As this vulnerability disencourages liquidators, the bad positions will remain under-collateralized.

### Mitigation

This finding sounds more suggestion than vulnerability.

However, as this is a serious problem in leveraged trading, I'd highly suggest adding liquidation bonus.
