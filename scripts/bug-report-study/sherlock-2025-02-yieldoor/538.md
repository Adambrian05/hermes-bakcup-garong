# feeRecipient Address Cannot Be Assigned, Causing 100% Fee Loss

### Summary

In the Leverager contract, the feeRecipient address cannot be assigned, preventing it from receiving liquidation fees. As a result, all fees are sent to the zero address (0x0), effectively burning them and causing a 100% loss of collected fees.

### Root Cause

In [Leverager.sol](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L47), the contract implementation prevents feeRecipient from being assigned.

### Internal Pre-conditions

none

### External Pre-conditions

Liquidation events generate fees, which the contract attempts to distribute.

### Attack Path

1.	A user triggers a liquidation.
2.	The contract generates fees and attempts to send them to feeRecipient.
3.	Since feeRecipient remains 0x0, the funds are lost permanently.

### Impact

100% loss of liquidation fees, leading to reduced protocol revenue.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Add a setter function, ensuring feeRecipient can be updated after deployment.
