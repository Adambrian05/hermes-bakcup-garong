# TWAP Inversion in Price Calculation will cause incorrect asset valuations

### Summary

Incorrect mathematical inversion of TWAP will cause inaccurate asset valuations for leveraged positions as users will face premature or delayed liquidations due to improper price calculations during market volatility.

### Root Cause

In `Leverager.sol:420-431`, the `_calculateTokenValues` function inverts the TWAP price mathematically (`PRECISION / price`) rather than using a properly calculated inverse TWAP, causing price calculation inaccuracies.

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L385-L401

```solidity
// If chPrice0 is 0, it means token0 doesnt have a pricefeed.
// Therefore, convert it token1 and from there, convert it to USD
if (chPrice0 == 0) {
    usdValue += (amount0 * price / PRECISION) * chPrice1 / decimals1;
} else if (chPrice1 == 0) {
    usdValue += amount1 * PRECISION / price * chPrice0 / decimals0;
}
```
The mathematical principle causing this issue is that the average of inverses is not equal to the inverse of averages. In volatile markets, a TWAP of token0/token1 prices inverted mathematically (1/TWAP) will diverge from a properly calculated TWAP of token1/token0 prices. This divergence directly impacts position valuations and liquidation thresholds for leveraged positions.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. One of the tokens in a leveraged position must not have a Chainlink price feed
2. The protocol must rely on TWAP price inversions for token value calculations
3. `_calculateTokenValues` must be called during price valuation (happens in both position opening and liquidation checks)


### External Pre-conditions

1. The market for the token pair must experience volatility sufficient to create a discernible difference between inverted TWAPs and inverse TWAPs 

### Attack Path

1. Attacker observes a volatile market condition for a token pair where one token lacks a Chainlink feed
2. Attacker opens a leveraged position with unfavorable collateralization ratio
3. Market volatility causes the mathematical inversion of TWAP to diverge from the true inverse TWAP
4. Due to inaccurate price calculation, the position either:
    - Avoids liquidation when it should be liquidated (if price calculation undervalues position risk)
    - Gets liquidated prematurely (if price calculation overvalues position risk)
5. The attacker can capitalize on either scenario by avoiding losses or liquidating others' positions


### Impact

The protocol and users suffer financial losses due to incorrect valuations of leveraged positions. In significant market volatility, mathematical inversion errors can lead to mispriced assets by up to 0.4-1.0%, potentially causing:

1. **For the protocol**: Accumulation of bad debt when positions that should be liquidated remain open due to undervaluation of risk
2. **For users**: Unfair liquidations when positions are incorrectly deemed undercollateralized due to overvaluation of risk

The impact is magnified in highly leveraged positions where small price calculation errors can trigger liquidation thresholds, causing direct losses to users.
