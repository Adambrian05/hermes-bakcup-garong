# Slot0 can be manipulated while `depositing` and `withdrawing`

### Summary

This issue arises while calculating `balance()`, it uses `slot0`, and there is no check, to check allowed `deviation`, : A manipulated price skews the reported totalBalance0 and totalBalance1, leading to incorrect share calculation during deposits.

### Root Cause

In `vault::deposit()` the `fees` are first collected, then `balance()` is called, on `balance()` uses `slot0` .
```javascript
 (uint160 sqrtPriceX96, , , , , , ) = IUniswapV3Pool(pool).slot0();
```
due to lack of check if `sqrtPriceX96` has deviated past the allowed amount, an attacker can manipulate, using flashloans  leading to mint to larger `shares` for the attacker,

### Attack Path


### Price Manipulation:

1. Attacker uses a flash loan to swap a large amount of tokens in the pool, moving the spot price to an extreme value.

- This causes the strategy to miscalculate the existing liquidity's value (e.g., showing lower balances than actual).

#### Deposit with Inflated Shares:

2. Attacker deposits tokens while the price is manipulated. The _calcDeposit function computes shares based on the manipulated (lower) pool value.

For example, if the real value is 100 ETH but manipulated to 50 ETH, depositing 10 ETH gives 50% shares instead of 10%.

#### Results

After the deposit, the price returns to normal. The attacker's shares now represent a larger portion of the true pool value, allowing them to withdraw more assets.

### Impact

After the hack, the `attacker`, can withdraw the with the inflated shares, leading to users lossing funds
