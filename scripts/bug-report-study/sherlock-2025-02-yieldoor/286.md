# Attackers manipulate prices in a short period of time.

### Summary

The contract utilizes the `twapPrice()` (Time-Weighted Average Price) to estimate the value of positions. However, if the TWAP calculation method is not robust enough, attackers can manipulate trading activities within a short timeframe, causing the `twapPrice()` to deviate from the true market price. Moreover, under conditions of low liquidity, the effectiveness of the `checkPoolActivity()` function may be compromised.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L113-L114
First using `twapPrice()` to calculate the price and then checking liquidity can lead to a situation where, under conditions of low liquidity, an attacker can influence the TWAP calculation through a series of small trades, causing it to overestimate or underestimate the price. However, by this point, the price has already been calculated. In the `checkPoolActivity()` function, there is also no mention of considerations regarding liquidity.
```solidity
tick = int24((nextCumulativeTick - tickCumulative) / int56(uint56(nextTimestamp - timestamp)));
```
The calculation of the tick is based on the rate of change between two adjacent ticks. This method does not directly examine the liquidity of the pool, such as the amount of funds (liquidity) within the pool. If the pool is emptied, price changes may still fall within the `maxObservationDeviation`, leading to erroneous judgments.

### Attack Path

* **Attack One**: The attacker can manipulate the tick with a relatively small amount of capital, causing the `twapPrice()` to deviate from the true market price.  
  The `checkPoolActivity()` function may mistakenly conclude that market volatility is low, as the attacker can control the tick movement to remain within the `maxObservationDeviation`.  

* **Attack Two**: The attacker manipulates the TWAP price to artificially inflate the value of the initial collateral during calculation. The attacker then opens a highly leveraged position (since `initCollateralUsd` is overestimated). When the TWAP price returns to normal, the position is automatically liquidated, and the attacker may profit through the liquidator mechanism.

### Impact

Disrupting the mechanism of leverage principles leads to inaccurate price calculations.

### PoC

no

### Mitigation

* Introduce multiple price sources (e.g., Chainlink Oracles) instead of relying solely on TWAP.
* Add a liquidity check before invoking `checkPoolActivity()` to avoid scenarios with poor liquidity:
```solidity
require(IUniswapV3Pool(pool).liquidity() > MIN_LIQUIDITY, "Low liquidity");
```
* Increase the TWAP calculation time window to reduce the possibility of short-term manipulation.
* Set an upper limit for price deviation to prevent sudden and significant price fluctuations.
