# Stale data check absent in `PriceFeed.sol`

### Summary

Stale data check is missing in the function `_getChainlinkPrice()` in the contract `PriceFeed.sol`. This may cause loss to the protocol.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/PriceFeed.sol#L57-L62

In the function `_getChainlinkPrice()`, check for stale data is missing and has been commented out.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. A leveraged position has become liquidateable.
2. `getPrice()` which in turn calls `_getChainlinkPrice()` gives stale data and says otherwise..

### External Pre-conditions

1. Chainlink pricefeed has experienced delay in price updation due to network congestion or outage.

### Attack Path

1. Chainlink pricefeed has experienced delay in price updation due to network congestion or outage.
2. A leveraged `position` has become `liquidateable`.
3. But pricefeed shows stale data and `isLiquidateable` shows `false`. 

### Impact

This may cause high overall impact at multiple places in the protocol. Most of the calculations may not give correct result and may cause immense loss to the protocol.
