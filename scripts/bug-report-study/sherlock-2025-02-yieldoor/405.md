# Potential Fund Lockup from Unsynced pullFunds(...) / pushFunds(...)

### Summary

The lack of enforced synchronization between pullFunds(...) and pushFunds(...) will cause liquidity depletion for depositors as a leverager can withdraw funds without ensuring their return, leading to a permanent reduction in the yToken contract's balance.

### Root Cause

The protocol does not enforce that:

pushFunds(...) must always follow pullFunds(...) within the same transaction or timeframe.

The returned amount matches the withdrawn amount.

The lending pool’s liquidity remains unchanged after a complete cycle of pull and push operations.

If the leverager fails to return funds due to transaction failure or malicious behavior, the pool’s liquidity is permanently reduced.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The leverager calls pullFunds(...) to withdraw tokens from the yToken contract.

The function completes the transfer without requiring a paired pushFunds(...) call.

Liquidity tracking remains unchanged despite funds being removed.



### External Pre-conditions



No mechanism ensures the leverager pushes back funds within a required timeframe.

The protocol does not impose penalties for failing to return funds after withdrawal.

### Attack Path

The leverager calls pullFunds(...), withdrawing amount of the underlying asset.

The function completes successfully, transferring the tokens to the leverager.

The leverager fails (or refuses) to call pushFunds(...), leaving the pool with reduced liquidity.

Depositors and borrowers interact with the protocol based on incorrect liquidity levels, leading to mispriced borrowing rates or withdrawal failures.

### Impact

This vulnerability can permanently reduce the available liquidity in the lending pool, affecting depositors who may be unable to withdraw their funds. Additionally, borrowing calculations may be based on incorrect liquidity levels, leading to financial instability within the protocol.
