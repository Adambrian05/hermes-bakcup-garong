# The protocol creates uneven playing field for liquidation.

### Summary

When a borrower `isLiquidateable`, both the liquidators and the borrower are in fray to secure the collateral. But liquidators need to pay a `liquidationFee`, whereas the borrower may secure the collateral at this point without any fee. It gives an edge to borrower.

### Root Cause

No `isLiquidateable` check while withdrawing. There must be a check and depending on it, an extra `liquidationFee` must be charged to the borrower/approved. This will ensure level playing field.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. A borrow `isLiquidateable`.

### External Pre-conditions

1. Borrower/approved(bot) and liquidator(bot) are in fray to secure the collateral.

### Attack Path

1. A borrow `isLiquidateable`.
2. Borrower/approved(bot) and liquidator(bot) are in fray to secure the collateral.
3. Borrower/approved(bot) has more incentives to front run liquidator(bot) and secure the collateral.
4. If borrower wins, loss to the protocol in terms of `liquidationFee`.
5. Creates a back channel for liquidator(bot) to collude with borrower to become approved(bot) and secure the collateral on behalf of borrower.

### Impact

1. Uneven liquidation benefits may discourage liquidators.
2. Encourage risky borrows.

_No response_

### Mitigation

1. Make the liquidation field even for everyone.
2. Introduce `liquidationFee` for borrowers for withdrawal below `liquidation` level.
