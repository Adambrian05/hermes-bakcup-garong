# A lot of positions may be instantly liquidatable after the contracts are unpaused

### Summary

Vault, Lending pool may be paused, which will not allow users to withdraw their positions in the Leverager. When they are unpaused, if the price has changed enough, a lot of liquidations will be triggered.

### Root Cause

In the Leverager there is no delay during which users can not be [liquidated](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L299) after unpausing the vault or lending pool to allow users to withdraw.

### Internal Pre-conditions

Contracts are paused.

### External Pre-conditions

Price drop in the collateral or increase in the borrowed asset.

### Attack Path

1. Users have opened leveraged positions.
2. Contracts are paused.
3. When contracts are resumed, users may be instantly liquidated.

### Impact

Users are unfairly liquidated and take losses.

### PoC

None.

### Mitigation

Set a time window for users to not be liquidated right after unpausing.
