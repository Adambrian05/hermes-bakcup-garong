# maxObservationDeviation is set too low

### Summary

The choice to set `maxObservationDeviation` to 100 ticks (~1%) in the Strategy contract will cause frequent rebalancing failures for the vault as the strategy will reject normal pool volatility.

### Root Cause

The choice to set `maxObservationDeviation = 100` in [Strategy.sol:constructor](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L109) is a mistake as it imposes an overly tight constraint of approximately 1% price movement between consecutive observations within the 5-minute TWAP period, which is insufficient for most of the crypto pools  where such movements are common.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The pool’s price (tick) needs to fluctuate by more than 100 ticks (~1%) between any two observations within the `twap = 300` seconds (5 minutes).


### External Pre-conditions

The underlying Uniswap V3 pool (e.g., ETH/USDC with 0.3% fee tier) needs to experience normal market volatility, such as a price change from $2500 to $2525 (~1%) within 5 minutes.

### Attack Path

This is a vulnerability path rather than an attack path, as it affects functionality rather than enabling exploitation:
1. The pool’s price moves by more than 1%  within 5 minutes due to market activity.
2. The strategy calls [checkPoolActivity()](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L310), which calculates a `delta` > 100 ticks between observations [here](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/b5a0f779dce4236b02665606adb610099451a51a/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L331).
3. `checkPoolActivity()` returns `false`, preventing the rebalancer from proceeding with rebalancing or compounding actions.

### Impact

The functions `rebalance()`, `compound()`, `addVestingPosition()` in strategy.sol cannot function in most of the circumstance as they contain `_priceWithinRange()` check which calls `checkPoolActivity()`. The contract effectively causes a DOS to itself.
This results in user funds sitting ideally in the strategy contract and they cannot be transferred to main or secondary posiitions

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

Increase maxObservationDeviation to a higher value, such as 200 ticks (~2%) or 300 ticks (~3%), to accommodate normal volatility in volatile pools while still filtering out extreme swings. Alternatively, make it configurable by the owner or dynamically adjustable based on the pool’s historical volatility or fee tier.
