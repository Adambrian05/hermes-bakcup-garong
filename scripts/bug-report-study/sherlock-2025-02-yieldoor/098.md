# `Strategy::addVestingPosition()` may be arbitraged due to leftover amounts being donated to the vault

### Summary

`Strategy::addVestingPosition()` [allocates](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L426) liquidity to the strategy/vault by adding liquidity to the uniswap pool. When doing this, there are leftover amounts from the liquidity addition due to price movements of the pool since the values were calculated offchain. In case the price movements are significant, a considerable amount of liquidity could be donated to the vault, up to the allowance of `_requirePriceWithinRange()`, which is around `100` ticks or `1%`. This price shift will lead to donations to the vault which can be completely arbitraged away by depositing, `Strategy::addVestingPosition()` is called and withdrawing. Even without frontrunning, it is something that may happen organically and users get a significant donation without needing to stay in the protocol.

### Root Cause

In `Strategy:426`, leftover liquidity may be arbitraged.

### Internal Pre-conditions

None.

### External Pre-conditions

Price movement before `Strategy::addVestingPosition()` leads to donation which is arbitraged away organically or maliciously.

### Attack Path

1. Offchain liquidity provision is calculated.
2. Swap happens, changing the pool price.
3. User deposits in the vault.
4. Add vesting position is called, donating a significant amount of funds to the vault.
5. User that deposited in 3 can withdraw for a profit without contributing anything.

### Impact

Arbitrage and profit for user that just deposited.

### PoC

See above.

### Mitigation

Handle the leftover amounts. Even if deposit fees are introduced, they are not enough in many cases.
