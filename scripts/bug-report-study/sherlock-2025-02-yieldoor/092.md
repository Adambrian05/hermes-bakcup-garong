# Strategy never checks for tick limits which will DoS it

### Summary

The strategy [allocates](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L240-L241) the main and secondary positions according to the current tick of a pool and some width to each side. Whenever
1. the current tick is near one of the tick limits (`+-887272`)
2. the width of the position is large
3. the spacing of the pool is large
The tick limit is likely to be hit, DoSing rebalancing and the bots. Any swap may cause this situation to happen so the bots never have complete control over this.

### Root Cause

In the strategy, the ticks limit are not checked when rebalancing.

### Internal Pre-conditions

Conditions mentioned in the summary.

### External Pre-conditions

None.

### Attack Path

1. Pool price movements make the tick limits be exceeded and rebalancing reverts, which is time sensitive and could miss significant fees in case of a big swap occuring.

### Impact

DoS, missing fees.

### PoC

1. Tick spacing may be up to 200
2. pool price may be anywhere
3. tick width may be large and there is no restriction.
4. Large swaps can instantly modify ticks by a significant amount.

### Mitigation

Cap the ticks of the positions to the minimum and maximum limits.
