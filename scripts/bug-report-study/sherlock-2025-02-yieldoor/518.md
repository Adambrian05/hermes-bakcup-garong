# user may suffer from liquidation loss due to lacking ways of increasing collaterals

### Summary

There is no way around in this protocol for leverage user tops up collaterals once the leverage position is opened.
Users may suffer from liquidation loss when market fluctuation high for a short period.

### Root Cause

The protocol allow users to open leverage position with leverages no more than max leverage limits.  Once the positions opened, there are no methods to raise collateral amount .
  If there is a short period price surge of borrowed token,  user may face a choice where he should withdraw his position immediately or liquidated by liquidation bot. Either way, user will suffer a loss of his initial value.


### Internal Pre-conditions

None

### External Pre-conditions

1.  borrowed token price surges.
2.  user's total deposited values are close to borrowed amount
3.  user should choose to either withdraw his position repay the borrowed tokens or wait to be liquidated
4.  user suffers loss from the short period price surging.

### Attack Path

None

### Impact

Users may suffer loss from a short period price surge of borrowed token.

### PoC

_No response_

### Mitigation

consider adding a method to allow user to increase their collaterals
