# Error in `Strategy.collectFees()`

### Summary

There is an error in the function `collectFees()` where `mainPosition.tickUpper` is used instead of `vestPosition.tickUpper`. It may make `vestingPosition` corrupt and impossible to withdraw from leading to loss of funds. Also, `collectFees()` consistently fails, causing `deposit()`, `withdraw()` and `addVestingPosition()` to revert.

When `rebalance()` is called while a `vestingPosition` is **ongoing**, `mainPosition.tickUpper` may change and no longer matches `vestPosition.tickUpper`. Consequently, when `collectFees()` is invoked, it encounters an `NP` error because the `sender`, `lowerTick`, and `upperTick` used to calculate the `vestingPosition` do not correctly reference the expected position. As a result, `collectFees()` consistently fails, causing `deposit()`, `withdraw()`, and `addVestingPosition()` to revert.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L166-L168

The use of `mainPosition.tickUpper` instead of `vestPosition.tickUpper` is an error.

### Internal Pre-conditions

1. `ongoingVestingPosition` must be `true`.
2. `onlyRebalancer` calls `rebalance()` after the `rebalanceInterval`.
3. `rebalance()` causes change in `mainPosition.tickUpper`.


### External Pre-conditions

1. `tickPrice` must change, such that `mainPosition.tickUpper` changes.

### Attack Path

1. `ongoingVestingPosition` must be `true`.
2. `onlyRebalancer` calls `rebalance()` after the `rebalanceInterval`.
3. `tickPrice` must change, such that `mainPosition.tickUpper` changes.
4. `rebalance()` causes change in `mainPosition.tickUpper`.

### Impact

1. Loss of access to `vestPosition` funds.
2. All functions where `collectFees()` is called will revert with `NP` error.

### Mitigation

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L166-L168

Replace `mainPosition.tickUpper` with `vestPosition.tickUpper`.
