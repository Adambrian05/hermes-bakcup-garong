# Withdrawing from positions in volatile market will cause bank run scenario and significant loss for prospective withdrawals and protocol instability

### Summary

Protocol witnesses  a critical vulnerability when users withdrawing their positions during volatile market conditions can trigger a bank run scenario, potentially causing significant losses to users who withdraw later and lead to protocol instability.


### Root Cause

Absence of pool activity check in the `withdraw` function in Leverage contract will cause users to lose significantly when they withdraw after an extreme market volatility. 

### Internal Pre-conditions

Alice, Bob, and Charlie each:
- Approve max spending of WBTC and USDC to the leverager contract.
- Open leveraged positions using ILeverager(leverager).openLeveragedPosition(lp).

Each position has:
Vault deposits: 0.5 WBTC and 50,000 USDC
Max borrowable amount: 0.6 WBTC
Denomination: WBTC (meaning the position is structured in WBTC terms)
Swap paths: Defined to route WBTC/USDC trades via WETH

- After 52 weeks, bob withdraws
He recovers nearly all of his USDC.
His WBTC withdrawal is rounded down to 99 wei (nearly nothing).
His USDC recovery is 200% of his original deposit.

- High market volatility happens
- Reverts when users make deposit in volatile market - see [Leverage.sol:L114](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L114)

### External Pre-conditions

Alice and Charlie withdraw after the market volatility event.
Their WBTC recovery drops massively, but their USDC recovery increases:
- Alice's WBTC recovery: 29,765,744 wei (59.53% of initial)
- Alice's USDC recovery: 75,000,000,000 wei (150% of initial)
Charlie’s WBTC & USDC recovery is almost identical to Alice’s.

### Attack Path

Market Volatility Event Happens - 100 WBTC for USDC on Uniswap

- It could be an attacker manipulating price with flashloans
- LP make larger swaps 100 WBTC for USDC on Uniswap

This changes the borrowing index and affects remaining leveraged positions.

### Impact

1. Attacker can manipulate price (e.g., flash loans) and withdraw unfairly from leeverage position.
2. If too many users withdraw in a volatile market, the pool may face a liquidity shortfall.

### Mitigation

1. Slippage protection for users withdrawing after a market volatility.
2. Introduce withdrawal restriction during extreme market volatility.
