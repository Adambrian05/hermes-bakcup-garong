# Vault depositors take guaranteed losses up to the slippage defined

### Summary

The vault USD value will be inflated whenever the pool is imbalanced, and is going to be arbitraged away and become lower at some point. When users deposit, they specify slippage protection, which mitigates the damage up to their limit, but they still take this loss.

### Root Cause

In `Vault:deposit()`, users will take losses when [depositing](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L48). 

### Internal Pre-conditions

None.

### External Pre-conditions

Pool is imbalanced (the more, the bigger the loss).

### Attack Path

1. User deposits in the vault at an imbalanced pool (any value above 0 leads to losses).

### Impact

User consistent losses.

### PoC

Suppose a pool has 1 ETH and 1000 USDC, but the price of ETH is 900 ETH.
The pool is imbalanced, which means arbitrages will get ETH out of it and reduce the pool value's.
If some user deposits when the pool is at 1 ETH and 1000 USDC, they will get less value out of their shares right away as soon as the pool is arbitraged.

### Mitigation

When the user deposits, they should get their shares from the USD price from an oracle, such that they never take these instant, unpredictable losses.
