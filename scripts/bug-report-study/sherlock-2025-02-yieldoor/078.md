# TWAP can be manipulated affecting `Leverager` functions

### Summary

The insufficient observation cardinality in Uniswap V3 pools can affect functions that depends on the TWAP price as an attacker can manipulate the TWAP price used for critical calculations.

### Root Cause

In `Strategy.sol`, the protocol relies on Uniswap V3 TWAP for price calculations but fails to ensure sufficient observation cardinality:

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L345-L361

However, two issues cause the TWAP to be easily manipulated:

1. Uniswap V3 pools are initialized with an observation cardinality of 1, which means that the TWAP oracle will only consider the last price update.
2. Even if the observation cardinality is high enough for the observation window, the number of observations recorded might not be enough.

This `twapPrice()` function is used in `Leverager.sol` to calculate the amount of collateral used and the amount of leverage that can be used. If the TWAP price is manipulated, the attacker can open a position with more leverage than intended.

### Internal Pre-conditions

N/A

### External Pre-conditions

1. The pool needs to have low observation cardinality (default is 1 for new pools)
2. The pool needs to have insufficient observations recorded within the TWAP window

### Attack Path

1. Attacker identifies a pool with low observation cardinality used by the protocol
2. Attacker executes a large swap to significantly move the price in their desired direction
3. The protocol calls `twapTick()` to calculate the TWAP price
4. The TWAP calculation returns the manipulated price due to insufficient observations

### Impact

The `Leverager::openLeveragedPosition()` uses the TWAP price to calculate the amount of collateral used and the amount of leverage that can be used. If the TWAP price is manipulated, the attacker can open a position with more leverage than intended.

The `Leverager::liquidatePosition()` uses the TWAP price to calculate the amount of collateral in usd value and compares it with the amount of borrowed tokens. If the TWAP price is manipulated, the attacker can liquidate a position that is not liquidatable.

### Mitigation

1. Call the `IUniswapV3Pool.increaseObservationCardinalityNext(uint16 observationCardinalityNext)` function after the initialization of the pool with a value high enough to cover the observation window. This value should be at least `observationWindow / averageBlockProductionRageOfTheNetwork`.
2. Ensure the number of observations recorded is high enough to cover the observation window.
