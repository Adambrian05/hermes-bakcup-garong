# Unfair distribution of shares for the first depositor.

### Summary

In `Vault::deposit` before minting shares we check for `totalSupply = 0` if its true we are minting `1000` shares to `BURN` which is `address(1337)`, and then mint shares to the depositor. Doing this is not needed as the first depositor will get the same amount of shares as the amount deposited but because of this depositor is loosing value worth 1000 shares. Even though the amount of shares lost is minuscule it is lost for no reason and it can be stopped. 

### Root Cause

[Vault.sol:67](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Vault.sol#L67) Here we are minting `1000` share to `BURN` for no reason causing loss of value to the first depositor.

### Internal Pre-conditions

This loss of shares happens only once and it happens in the first depositor.

### External Pre-conditions

NA

### Attack Path

- `Owner` sets up the protocol.
- `user` who is also the first depositor deposits `1000 e6 USDC` and `0.01 e18 WBTC`.
- After calculation of `users` shares `1000 shares` are sent to `BURN` because the `totalSupply` of shares is `0` after that users shares are minted 
- The user gets `0.01 e18 - 1000` according to the calculation of shares.
- The total totalSupply would be `0.01 e18` but as the only depositor at this point `user` should have all the shares but because of the unnecessary minting of tokens to the `BURN` `user` losser value.

### Impact

- Unfair distribution of shares to the first depositor.
- Loss of value for the first depositor.
- The lost funds can never be retrieved.
