# `tickTwapDeviation`, `maxObservationDeviation`, `twap` should be set in a per pool/asset basis

### Summary

`tickTwapDeviation`, `maxObservationDeviation`, `twap` are set the [same](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L100-L109) in the constructor for all pools and assets, which is inaccurate as assets have different volatility levels and will lead to being too restrictive for some assets and reverting or letting too much deviation for others. It will lead to loss of fees because the ability to make sure the price is not manipulated will be compromised and revert or allow imbalanced pools for some assets.

### Root Cause

In the strategy, the same `tickTwapDeviation`, `maxObservationDeviation` and `twap` are used for all pools in the constructor.

### Internal Pre-conditions

None.

### External Pre-conditions

None.

### Attack Path

1. Protocol rebalances, compounds or adds a vested position in an imbalanced pool due to lax twap or fails to do so due to restrictive twap parameters.

### Impact

Loss of fees for users and protocol as the rebalance, compound and adding vested positions mechanism are compromised.

### PoC

Pools with for example tick spacings of 200, have very different tick deviation requirements of pools with a tick spacing of 1. A tick spacing of 1 means the price would have to cross 200 liquidity borders to reach a deviation of 200. However, a tick spacing of 200 means only 1 tick boundary crossing is enough to reach the limit. 

### Mitigation

Set these parameters in the constructor without hardcoding them.
