# Incorrect Repayment Logic in withdraw() in the Leverager.sol contract

### Summary

Bug in repayment calculation in the `Leverager.sol::withdraw()` function.
in `Leverager.sol` the wrong variable is used inside the ternary condition.

### Root Cause

https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Leverager.sol#L231-L235
this should check `amountOut1` ( because `up.token1` is the borrowed token) and instead, it incorrectly assigns `amountOut0` in the ternary condition.

### Internal Pre-conditions

The attacker need to open a leveraged position where `token1` is the borrowed asset calling the `Leverager.col::withdraw()` function.


### External Pre-conditions

There is no external pre-condition as far as i understand this field.

### Attack Path

1. The attacker need to open a leveraged position where `token1` is the borrowed asset.
2. The attacker withdraw a large amount of `token1` ensuring `amountOut1>amountOut0`
3. The attacker underpays the loan and receive a large amount of `token1`(swap into an other token.
5. At then end, the protocol has a unpaid debt.

### Impact

Loss of Funds: the lending pool may have to cover the unpaid debt, and the attacker can drain liquidity.
