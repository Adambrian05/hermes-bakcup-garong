# Funds in vesting positions remain unprotected from price drift


## Summary
The `rebalance()` function in `Strategy.sol` fails to include vesting position funds in its rebalancing operations. This oversight leaves vesting positions vulnerable to price movements and potentially reduces yield for both the protocol and users.

## Root Cause
In [`Strategy.sol#L198-L215`](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2025-02-yieldoor/blob/main/yieldoor/src/Strategy.sol#L198-L215), the `rebalance()` function only handles main and secondary positions, omitting vesting positions:

```solidity
    function rebalance() public onlyRebalancer {
        require(block.timestamp - lastRebalance >= rebalanceInterval, "too soon since last rebalance");

        _requirePriceWithinRange(); // we verify that the pool's price is currently not manipulated
        collectFees();

        _removeLiquidity();
        (uint256 amount0, uint256 amount1) = idleBalances();

        (uint160 sqrtPriceX96, int24 tick,,,,,) = IUniswapV3Pool(pool).slot0();

        _setMainTicks(tick);
        (amount0, amount1) = _addLiquidityToMainPosition(sqrtPriceX96, amount0, amount1);

        _setSecondaryPositionsTicks(tick);
        _addLiquidityToSecondaryPosition(sqrtPriceX96, amount0, amount1);
        lastRebalance = block.timestamp;
    }
```

The function:
1. Removes liquidity from main and secondary positions
2. Updates tick ranges based on current price
3. Redeposits liquidity to optimized ranges
4. Completely ignores vesting positions, leaving them vulnerable to price drift

## Internal pre-conditions
1. Active vesting position exists with significant liquidity
2. Vesting position's tick range becomes suboptimal due to price movement
3. Rebalancing interval has elapsed (`block.timestamp - lastRebalance >= rebalanceInterval`)

## External pre-conditions
1. Pool price has moved significantly from vesting position's original range
2. Price movement is gradual enough to pass `_requirePriceWithinRange()` checks
3. Market conditions warrant rebalancing of positions

## Attack Path
1. Owner deposits funds to vesting position with specific tick range
2. Market price gradually moves away from vesting position's range
3. Rebalancer executes `rebalance()`
4. Main and secondary positions are optimized for new price
5. Vesting position remains in original, now suboptimal range
6. Yield is lost due to:
   - Position being out of active trading range
   - Reduced fee collection
   - Inefficient capital utilization

## Impact
The lack of vesting position rebalancing leads to:
1. Reduced yield for vesting position funds
2. Capital inefficiency in the protocol
3. Potential for complete yield loss if price moves significantly
4. Misalignment between vesting position and current market conditions

## Mitigation
1. Include vesting positions in rebalancing operations:
   - Consider remaining vesting duration
   - Maintain proportional distribution
   - Preserve vesting schedule
2. Add safety checks for vesting position rebalancing:
   - Minimum price deviation threshold
   - Maximum rebalancing frequency
   - Slippage protection
