# The `maxTimesLeverage` of the Vault and the `maxLevTimes` of the LendingPool

### Summary
The `maxTimesLeverage` of the Vault and the `maxLevTimes` of the LendingPool are not being utilized correctly.

### Root Cause
Users can open leverage positions based on the minimum of `vp.maxTimesLeverage` and `maxLevTimes`. 
However, in the `isLiquidatable` function, only `vp.maxTimesLeverage` is considered

### PoC
Assuming:
`Vault's maxTimesLeverage := 5`, `LendingPool's maxLevTimes := 2`
If the prices of collaterals and borrowed assets all decrease, the `base` in line 408 could be used instead of `pos.initCollateralValue` in the `isLiquidatable` function.
Users should only be able to open 2x leverage positions and not 5x leverage positions. 
However, in line 408, 5x is considered instead of 2x, resulting in a decreased liquidation threshold.

While there are additional issues to address, using `min(vp.maxTimesLeverage, maxLevTimes)` instead of solely using `vp.maxTimesLeverage` is necessary.

### Impact
The incorrect liquidation threshold can lead to unintended losses or gains for leverage users and the protocol.

### Mitigation
Consider using `min(vp.maxTimesLeverage, maxLevTimes)` instead of solely using `vp.maxTimesLeverage` in the `isLiquidatable` function.
